# Sound-Alike & Similar-Meaning Words (同音異義語・同訓異字)

Japanese phonology features a relatively small phonetic inventory (around 100 morae) mapped onto tens of thousands of Sino-Japanese compounds and native verbal roots. Consequently, **homophones (同音異義語 / *dōon igigo*)** and **kanji sharing identical native readings (同訓異字 / *dōkun iji*)** are among the most frequently tested vocabulary traps on the **JLPT N3, N2, and N1**.

---

## 1. Multi-Kanji Native Verb Sets (同訓異字 / Dōkun Iji)

These native Japanese verbs share the exact same pronunciation and basic concept, but use different kanji depending on the specific object or domain:

### A. The 4 "Osameru" Verbs (おさめる)

| Kanji | Reading | Core Meaning / Nuance | High-Frequency Real-World Collocations |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **収める** | おさめる | **Store away / Obtain / Receive** (putting something into a collection, obtaining profit, achieving success) | • 成功を**収める** (*achieve great success*)<br>• 利益を**収める** (*reap profits*)<br>• 刀を鞘に**収める** (*sheathe a sword*) |
| **納める** | おさめる | **Deliver / Pay dues / Supply** (fulfilling an obligation, paying taxes or tuition) | • 税金を**納める** (*pay taxes*)<br>• 授業料を**納める** (*pay tuition fees*)<br>• 注文の品を**納める** (*deliver ordered goods*) |
| **治める** | おさめる | **Govern / Pacify / Quell** (ruling a country, managing social disorder, bringing peace) | • 国を**治める** (*govern a nation*)<br>• 暴動を**治める** (*quell a riot*)<br>• 争いを**治める** (*settle a dispute*) |
| **修める** | おさめる | **Master / Cultivate / Study** (disciplining conduct, completing an academic course) | • 学問を**修める** (*master an academic discipline*)<br>• 身を**修める** (*discipline one's conduct*) |

## 2. High-Frequency Sino-Japanese Homophones (同音異義語 / Dōon Igigo)

### A. たいしょう (*Taishō*)

| Kanji | Pitch Accent | Meaning | Typical Usage Context |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **対象** | 0 (Heiban) | **Target / Subject of study or action** | • 調査の**対象** (*the subject group of a survey*)<br>• 課税の**対象** (*subject to taxation*) |
| **対照** | 0 (Heiban) | **Contrast / Comparison of differences** | • **対照的**な性格 (*contrasting personalities*)<br>• 原本と**対照**する (*cross-check against the original*) |
| **対称** | 0 (Heiban) | **Symmetry (Geometric or structural balance)** | • 左右**対称** (*bilateral symmetry*)<br>• **対称**軸 (*axis of symmetry*) |
