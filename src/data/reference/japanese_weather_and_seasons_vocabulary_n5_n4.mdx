# Japanese Weather & Seasons Vocabulary (天気と季節)

Japan has four distinct and culturally celebrated seasons (**四季 / *shiki*)**. Daily weather expressions, forecasts (**天気予報 / *tenki yohō*)**, and temperature words are fundamental components of polite small talk and JLPT listening and reading comprehension.

---

## 1. The Four Seasons (四季 / Shiki)

| Season | Kanji | Reading | Romaji | Months (Approx.) | Seasonal Phenomena & Key Words |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spring** | **春** | はる | *haru* | March – May | **桜** (*sakura* - cherry blossoms), **お花見** (*ohanami* - flower viewing), **暖かい** (*atatakai* - pleasantly warm), **花粉症** (*kafunshō* - pollen allergy) |
| **Summer** | **夏** | なつ | *natsu* | June – August | **梅雨** (*tsuyu* - rainy season), **蒸し暑い** (*mushiatsui* - humid), **台風** (*taifū* - typhoon), **花火大会** (*hanabi taikai* - fireworks festival) |
| **Autumn** | **秋** | あき | *aki* | September – November | **紅葉** (*kōyō / momiji* - autumn foliage), **涼しい** (*suzushii* - pleasantly cool), **読書の秋** (*reading autumn*), **食欲の秋** (*appetite autumn*) |
| **Winter** | **冬** | ふゆ | *fuyu* | December – February | **雪** (*yuki* - snow), **寒い** (*samui* - cold weather), **氷** (*kōri* - ice), **暖房** (*danbō* - heating), **お正月** (*oshōgatsu* - New Year) |

## 2. Weather Conditions (天気の表現)

| Condition | Noun Form | Verb / Phrasal Form | Meaning | Sample Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sunny / Clear** | **晴れ** (*hare*) | **晴れる** (*hareru*) | To clear up / become sunny | 明日は晴れるでしょう。<br>*It will probably be sunny tomorrow.* |
| **Cloudy** | **曇り** (*kumori*) | **曇る** (*kumoru*) | To become overcast/cloudy | 午後から曇ってきました。<br>*It became cloudy starting in the afternoon.* |
| **Rain** | **雨** (*ame*) | **雨が降る** (*ame ga furu*) | Rain falls | 外は雨が降っています。<br>*It is raining outside.* |
| | | **雨が止む** (*ame ga yamu*) | Rain stops | やっと雨が止んだ。<br>*Finally, the rain stopped.* |
| **Snow** | **雪** (*yuki*) | **雪が降る** (*yuki ga furu*) | Snow falls | 東京で雪が降りました。<br>*It snowed in Tokyo.* |
| **Wind** | **風** (*kaze*) | **風が吹く** (*kaze ga fuku*) | Wind blows | 強い風が吹いています。<br>*A strong wind is blowing.* |
| **Thunder** | **雷** (*kaminari*) | **雷が鳴る** (*kaminari ga naru*) | Thunder rumbles / strikes | 雷が鳴り始めた。<br>*Thunder began to rumble.* |
| **Rainbow** | **虹** (*niji*) | **虹が出る** (*niji ga deru*) | A rainbow appears | 雨の後に虹が出た。<br>*A rainbow appeared after the rain.* |
| **Typhoon** | **台風** (*taifū*) | **台風が近づく / 上陸する** | Typhoon approaches / makes landfall | 台風が接近しています。<br>*A typhoon is approaching.* |

## 3. The Two Pairs of Hot and Cold (Crucial JLPT Trap!)

Japanese strictly distinguishes between **ambient atmospheric temperature (weather)** and **the temperature of tangible physical objects (water, food, touching surfaces)**:

```
                  [Ambient Weather]            [Physical Object / Liquid]
HOT   :           暑い (あつい / atsui)   ≠    熱い (あつい / atsui)
COLD  :           寒い (さむい / samui)   ≠    冷たい (つめたい / tsumetai)
```

| Dimension | Ambient Weather (Weather / Air) | Physical Object / Liquid (Touch) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HOT** | **暑い (あつい / *atsui*)**<br>今日はとても**暑い**ですね。<br>(*Today is very hot, isn't it?*) | **熱い (あつい / *atsui*)**<br>このお茶はとても**熱い**ですから気をつけて。<br>(*This tea is very hot, so be careful.*) |
| **COLD** | **寒い (さむい / *samui*)**<br>冬の朝は本当に**寒い**。<br>(*Winter mornings are truly cold.*) | **冷たい (つめたい / *tsumetai*)**<br>**冷たい**水を一杯ください。<br>(*Please give me a glass of cold water.*) |

> [!WARNING] Never use 寒い for Drinks or Objects!
> Asking for \*寒い水 (\*samui mizu) is grammatically incorrect in Japanese. Always use **冷たい水 (*tsumetai mizu*)**. Similarly, never say a room is \*冷たい; say the room's air is **寒い (*samui*)**!

## 4. Pleasant Comfort & Humidity

- **暖かい / 温かい (あたたかい / *atatakai*)**: Pleasantly warm.
  - **暖かい**: Weather / climate (e.g., 暖かい春 *warm spring*).
  - **温かい**: Warm food / heart / soup (e.g., 温かいスープ *warm soup*, 温かい心 *warm heart*).
- **涼しい (すずしい / *suzushii*)**: Pleasantly cool (e.g., 秋の夜は涼しい *autumn nights are pleasantly cool*).
- **蒸し暑い (むしあつい / *mushiatsui*)**: Muggy / humid and oppressive (typical Japanese summer).
- **湿度 (しつど / *shitsudo*)**: Humidity level (e.g., 湿度が高い *humidity is high*).
- **乾燥する (かんそうする / *kansō suru*)**: To dry out / arid air (common in winter: 空気が乾燥している).

## 5. Weather Forecast Expressions (天気予報の言葉)

In television and radio broadcasts, meteorologists use formal predictive structures:

- **最高気温 (さいこうきおん / *saikō kion*)**: Highest forecasted temperature.
- **最低気温 (さいていきおん / *saitei kion*)**: Lowest forecasted temperature.
- **降水確率 (こうすいかくりつ / *kōsui kakuritsu*)**: Probability of precipitation (e.g., 降水確率は80%です *probability of rain is 80%*).
- **時々 (ときどき / *tokidoki*)**: "At times / occasionally" (e.g., **曇り時々雨** *cloudy with occasional rain*).
- **のち (後 / *nochi*)**: "Later followed by" (e.g., **晴れのち雨** *sunny, followed later by rain*).
