# Noun Modifiers and Relative Clauses (連体修飾 / Rentai Shūshoku)

In English, modifying clauses follow the noun using relative pronouns:  
*"The book [**that** I bought yesterday]."*

Japanese, however, is strictly **head-final**. Modifying phrases and relative clauses **always precede the noun they describe**, and **relative pronouns (who, which, that, where) do not exist**:

```
English:   The book  ──►  [that I bought yesterday]
Japanese:  [昨日、私が買った]  ──►  本 (hon)
```

---

## 1. Noun Modification by Word Class

To attach any modifier to a noun, use its **attributive form (連体形 / *rentaikei*)**:

| Word Class | Rule | Example | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verb** | **Plain Form** (dict., た, ない, なかった) directly + Noun | **本を読む**人<br/>**昨日読んだ**本<br/>**肉を食べない**人 | the person who reads books<br/>the book I read yesterday<br/>a person who doesn't eat meat |
| **い-Adjective** | **Dictionary Form** directly + Noun | **新しい**靴<br/>**美味しかった**料理 | new shoes<br/>the dish that was delicious |
| **な-Adjective** | Stem + **な** + Noun | **静かな**町<br/>**有名だった**画家 | a quiet town<br/>a painter who was famous |
| **Noun** | Noun + **の** + Noun | **英語の**先生<br/>**去年の**冬 | an English teacher<br/>last year's winter |

---

## 2. The Internal Subject Rule: が (*ga*) vs. の (*no*)

Inside any relative clause, the grammatical subject carrying out the action must be marked by **が**, **never by は**:

- Incorrect: \*[私**は**作った] ケーキ  
- Correct: **[私が作った] ケーキ** (*The cake that I made*)
- Incorrect: \*[彼**は**住んでいる] アパート  
- Correct: **[彼が住んでいる] アパート** (*The apartment where he lives*)

### The が-to-の Conversion Rule (Ga/No Interchange)
In written and natural spoken Japanese, when a relative clause is short and direct, the subject marker **が** can be replaced by **の**:

- **[私が買った] 本** ＝ **[私の買った] 本**  
  *Watashi ga katta hon = Watashi no katta hon*  
  (The book that I bought)
- **[雨の降る] 日** ＝ **[雨が降る] 日**  
  *Ame no furu hi = Ame ga furu hi*  
  (A rainy day / A day when rain falls)

> [!NOTE]
> Avoid replacing が with の if the clause contains another object marked by を, as multiple の particles create ambiguity.

## 3. The 4 Typical Syntactic Gaps

Because Japanese lacks relative pronouns (*who, that, which, where*), the head noun can represent any grammatical role inside the modifying clause:

### 1. Subject Gap (The noun is the one performing the action)
- [公園で**走っている**] **人**  
  *Kōen de hashitte iru hito*  
  (The person [who] is running in the park)

### 2. Direct Object Gap (The noun is what is acted upon)
- [昨日**買った**] **鞄**  
  *Kinō katta kaban*  
  (The bag [that] I bought yesterday)

### 3. Location Gap (The noun is where the event occurred)
- [先週私たちが**泊まった**] **ホテル**  
  *Senshū watashitachi ga tomatta hoteru*  
  (The hotel [where] we stayed last week)

### 4. Time Gap (The noun is when the event happened)
- [父が**生まれた**] **年**  
  *Chichi ga umareta toshi*  
  (The year [when] my father was born)

## 4. Generic Head Nouns and Nominalizers

Relative clauses often attach to abstract nouns to build broader grammatical structures:

| Head Noun | Function | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **こと** | Abstract action / conceptual fact | "The fact/act of..." | 毎朝走る**こと**は健康にいい。<br/>(*The act of running every morning is good for health.*) |
| **の** | Concrete, tangible scene / entity | "The one / thing that..." | 赤い靴を履いている**の**が田中さんです。<br/>(*The one wearing red shoes is Tanaka.*) |
| **とき** | Temporal clause | "When / At the time that..." | 日本へ行く**とき**、パスポートが必要です。<br/>(*When you go to Japan, a passport is required.*) |
| **ところ** | Point in time / physical spot | "Just about to / Just did" | 今から出かける**ところ**です。<br/>(*I am just on the verge of heading out.*) |

## 5. Decoding Complex Nested Clauses (JLPT N3 Strategy)

In advanced reading passages, Japanese sentences frequently contain relative clauses nested inside other relative clauses:

> **[私が [昨日友達に借りた] 本] は、とても面白かったです。**

### Reading Strategy
1. Locate the **predicates** and their immediately following **nouns**.
2. Work from the **inside brackets outward**:
   - `昨日友達に借りた` (borrowed from a friend yesterday) ──► modifies `本` (book).
   - `私が [その本] を借りた` ──► The book that I borrowed from a friend yesterday.
   - `その本はとても面白かった` ──► Was very interesting!
