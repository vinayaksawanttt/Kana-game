# Japanese Verb Groups

Japanese verbs are usually divided into three groups. Group membership matters because the verb's final kana determines how it changes in forms such as **ます**, **て**, and **ない**.

| Group | Common Japanese name | Basic behavior | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Group 1 | 五段動詞 (*godan*, often called う-verbs) | The final kana moves among the five vowel rows. | 書く, 飲む, 話す |
| Group 2 | 一段動詞 (*ichidan*, often called る-verbs) | Remove final る, then attach the ending. | 食べる, 見る, 起きる |
| Group 3 | Irregular verbs | A small set has special conjugations. | する, 来る |

## 1. Group 1: う-Verbs (五段動詞)

The dictionary form ends in an う-row kana: う, く, ぐ, す, つ, ぬ, ぶ, む, or る. The ending changes depending on the conjugation.

| Dictionary form | Meaning | ます form | ない form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 買う (*kau*) | buy | 買います | 買わない |
| 書く (*kaku*) | write | 書きます | 書かない |
| 泳ぐ (*oyogu*) | swim | 泳ぎます | 泳がない |
| 話す (*hanasu*) | speak | 話します | 話さない |
| 待つ (*matsu*) | wait | 待ちます | 待たない |
| 死ぬ (*shinu*) | die | 死にます | 死なない |
| 遊ぶ (*asobu*) | play | 遊びます | 遊ばない |
| 飲む (*nomu*) | drink | 飲みます | 飲まない |
| 帰る (*kaeru*) | return | 帰ります | 帰らない |

The negative form of a verb ending in **う** uses **わない**, not *あない*: 買う → 買わない.

## 2. Group 2: る-Verbs (一段動詞)

Many Group 2 verbs end in **る**, and the kana immediately before る is commonly **い** or **え**. Remove る and add the needed ending:

| Dictionary form | Remove る | ます form | ない form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 食べる (*taberu*, eat) | 食べ | 食べます | 食べない |
| 見る (*miru*, see) | 見 | 見ます | 見ない |
| 起きる (*okiru*, get up) | 起き | 起きます | 起きない |
| 教える (*oshieru*, teach/tell) | 教え | 教えます | 教えない |

### The る-ending trap

Not every verb ending in る is a Group 2 verb. Many are Group 1 and must keep the る when forming the ます stem:

| Group 1 verb | Meaning | ます form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 帰る (*kaeru*) | return | 帰ります |
| 入る (*hairu*) | enter | 入ります |
| 走る (*hashiru*) | run | 走ります |
| 切る (*kiru*) | cut | 切ります |
| 知る (*shiru*) | know | 知ります |

The sound before る is a useful clue, not a perfect test. Learn the group with each new verb and use a dictionary when uncertain. Compare **着る** (*kiru*, put on; Group 2) with **切る** (*kiru*, cut; Group 1).

## 3. Group 3: Irregular Verbs

| Dictionary form | Meaning | ます form | ない form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| する | do | します | しない |
| 来る (くる) | come | 来ます (きます) | 来ない (こない) |

Many nouns form verbs with する: 勉強する (study), 料理する (cook), and 運動する (exercise). Their forms follow する: 勉強します, 勉強しない.

### Special negative: ある

**ある** (exist; for things) is a Group 1 verb in most forms, but its plain negative is irregular: **ない**, not *あらない*. Its polite negative is **ありません**.

## 4. A quick group-identification routine

1. Check whether the verb is **する** or **来る**; these are irregular.
2. If it ends in る, check whether you know it as Group 2 or Group 1. The preceding い/え sound is only a clue.
3. If it ends in another う-row kana, it is normally Group 1.
4. Record the dictionary form, group, and a useful conjugation together.

> **Learning tip:** Group names predict conjugation patterns, not meaning. A verb's group cannot reliably be guessed from its English translation.
