# JLPT N3 Grammar Patterns: Master Reference Sheet

The **JLPT N3** level marks the transition into intermediate Japanese. It centers on **compound particles** (prepositional phrases built on verbs), nuanced **logical justifications** (the **わけ** family), **tendencies and appearances** (がち, っぽい, 気味), and **emotional obligations**.

---

## 1. Compound Particles & Relational Frames

| Pattern | Formation | Core Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜について** | Noun + について | *About / Regarding [topic]* | 日本の文化**について**レポートを書きました。<br/>(*I wrote a report about Japanese culture.*) |
| **〜に対して** | Noun + に対して | *Toward [someone] / In contrast to* | 目上の人**に対して**丁寧な言葉を使います。<br/>(*Use polite language toward superiors.*)<br/>兄は陽気だが、弟**に対して**おとなしい。<br/>(*The older brother is cheerful, in contrast to the quiet younger brother.*) |
| **〜にとって** | Noun + にとって | *For / From the viewpoint of* | 留学生**にとって**、漢字は難しい課題だ。<br/>(*For international students, kanji is a difficult challenge.*) |
| **〜として** | Noun + として | *As / In the capacity or role of* | 彼は日本代表**として**オリンピックに出場した。<br/>(*He competed in the Olympics as a representative of Japan.*) |
| **〜によって** | Noun + によって | 1. *By means of / Through*<br/>2. *Due to / Because of*<br/>3. *Depending on* | インターネット**によって**世界と繋がる。<br/>(*Connected to the world by means of the internet.*)<br/>人**によって**考え方が違う。<br/>(*Ways of thinking differ depending on the person.*) |
| **〜に関して** | Noun + に関して | *Concerning / In connection with (more formal than について)* | この事件**に関して**、詳しい調査が行われている。<br/>(*Detailed investigations are underway concerning this incident.*) |

## 2. Boundaries, Span & Extent

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜にわたって** | Noun (time/space) + にわたって | *Spanning across / Throughout the entire range* | 会議は3日間**にわたって**行われた。<br/>(*The conference was held spanning over 3 days.*) |
| **〜を通じて / を通して** | Noun + を通じて / を通して | *1. Throughout the whole period*<br/>*2. Through the medium/agency of* | 四季**を通じて**花が咲いている。<br/>(*Flowers bloom throughout all four seasons.*)<br/>友人を**通して**彼と知り合った。<br/>(*I got to know him through a mutual friend.*) |
| **〜から〜にかけて** | Noun 1 + から + Noun 2 + にかけて | *From [A] through [B] (fuzzy, continuous boundary)* | 今夜から明日の朝**にかけて**大雨になるでしょう。<br/>(*Heavy rain is expected from tonight through tomorrow morning.*) |
| **〜をはじめ(として)** | Noun + をはじめ(として) | *Starting with [A] (as a representative example among many)* | 東京**をはじめとして**、全国の大都市で上映される。<br/>(*It will be screened in major cities nationwide, starting with Tokyo.*) |
| **〜ばかりでなく** | Plain + ばかりでなく / Noun | *Not only [A], but also [B]* | 彼は英語**ばかりでなく**、中国語も話せる。<br/>(*He speaks not only English, but also Chinese as well.*) |

## 3. The わけ (*Wake*) Family: Logical Reasoning

The noun **わけ** (*reason, sense, logic*) anchors four vital N3 grammar structures:

| Pattern | Formula | Nuance | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜わけだ** | Plain Form + わけだ | *No wonder / Naturally, it means that...* | 5年も日本にいたから、日本語が上手な**わけだ**。<br/>(*He was in Japan for 5 years, so no wonder his Japanese is good!*) |
| **〜わけがない** | Plain Form + わけがない | *There is no way that / It is impossible that...* | 一度も練習していないのに、できる**わけがない**。<br/>(*Having not practiced even once, there's no way you can do it!*) |
| **〜わけではない** | Plain Form + わけではない | *It doesn't mean that / Not necessarily the case* | 嫌いな**わけではない**が、あまり食べない。<br/>(*It's not that I dislike it, but I don't eat it often.*) |
| **〜わけにはいかない** | Verb [dict. / ない] + わけにはいかない | *Cannot afford to / Cannot do on moral or social grounds* | 大事な会議だから、休む**わけにはいかない**。<br/>(*Because it's a vital meeting, I cannot afford to be absent.*) |

## 4. Tendencies, Likeness & Negative Propensities

| Pattern | Formation | Nuance | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜がち** | Verb [ます-stem] + がち<br/>Noun + がち | *Tends to / Frequently prone to (negative habit)* | 冬は風邪をひき**がち**だ。<br/>(*In winter, one tends to catch colds.*)<br/>最近、遅刻し**がち**だ。<br/>(*Lately, he tends to be late.*) |
| **〜気味 (ぎみ)** | Verb [ます-stem] + 気味<br/>Noun + 気味 | *A slight touch of / A hint of (temporary physical/mental state)* | 少し風邪**気味**で、喉が痛い。<br/>(*I have a slight touch of a cold, and my throat hurts.*) |
| **〜っぽい** | Noun / Adj stem / Verb [ます-stem] + っぽい | *Resembling / -ish / Prone to (often negative appearance/trait)* | あの大人は子ども**っぽい**。<br/>(*That adult acts childish.*)<br/>彼は怒り**っぽい**。 (*He is quick-tempered / easily angered.*) |

## 5. Conviction, Repercussion & Emotion

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜に違いない** | Plain Form + に違いない (Noun direct) | *Must be / Bound to be (strong conviction)* | 犯人は彼**に違いない**。<br/>(*The culprit must be him, without doubt!*) |
| **〜にすぎない** | Plain Form + にすぎない (Noun direct) | *Merely / Nothing more than / Just* | これは単なる噂**にすぎない**。<br/>(*This is merely a rumor.*) |
| **〜たびに** | Verb [dict.] + たびに / Noun + のたびに | *Every time [A] happens, [B] occurs* | この写真を見る**たびに**、故郷を思い出す。<br/>(*Every time I look at this photo, I recall my hometown.*) |
| **〜最中に (さいちゅうに)** | Verb [ている] + 最中に / Noun + の最中に | *Right in the middle of [an unexpected interruption happens]* | 食事の**最中に**電話がかかってきた。<br/>(*Right in the middle of the meal, a phone call rang.*) |
| **〜てたまらない / 〜てしょうがない** | Verb [て-form] / Adj [て-form] + たまらない | *Unbearably [Adj] / Cannot help feeling* | 家族に会いたく**てたまらない**。<br/>(*I miss my family so much I can hardly stand it.*) |
| **〜べきだ / べきではない** | Verb [dict.] + べきだ (する → すべき) | *Should / Ought to (moral or social obligation)* | 約束は守る**べきだ**。<br/>(*One ought to keep promises.*) |
