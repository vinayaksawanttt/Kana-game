# On'yomi vs. Kun'yomi: Rules, Patterns & Exceptions (音読み・訓読み)

When kanji characters were imported into Japan from China between the 4th and 8th centuries, the Japanese people mapped Chinese characters onto their preexisting spoken language (*Yamato-kotoba*). As a result, almost every kanji possesses two distinct reading systems:

1. **On'yomi (音読み / Sino-Japanese Reading)**: Approximations of the ancient Chinese pronunciation at the time of importation.
2. **Kun'yomi (訓読み / Native Japanese Reading)**: The native Japanese word expressing that character's conceptual meaning.

---

## 1. The 4 Predictive Rules of Kanji Readings

While Japanese reading rules have exceptions, observing a word's morphological structure lets you predict its reading with **over 85% accuracy**:

| Word Structure | Typical Reading | Rationale | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Standalone Single Kanji** (No okurigana) | **Kun'yomi** | Functions as an independent native noun. | **山** (*yama* - mountain)<br>**川** (*kawa* - river)<br>**水** (*mizu* - water)<br>**猫** (*neko* - cat) |
| **2. Kanji + Okurigana** (Trailing Hiragana) | **Kun'yomi** | Native verbs and adjectives inflecting through kana. | **食**べる (*ta-beru*)<br>**新**しい (*atara-shii*)<br>**行**く (*i-ku*)<br>**静**か (*shizu-ka*) |
| **3. Multi-Kanji Compound (熟語 / Jukugo)** | **On'yomi + On'yomi** | Imported academic, bureaucratic, or technical terms. | **電車** (*den-sha*)<br>**先生** (*sen-sei*)<br>**図書館** (*to-sho-kan*)<br>**経済** (*kei-zai*) |
| **4. Reduplication (々) & Everyday Nature** | **Kun'yomi + Kun'yomi** | Ancient native poetic compounds (Rendaku applied). | **人々** (*hito-bito*)<br>**手紙** (*te-gami*)<br>**青空** (*ao-zora*)<br>**花火** (*hana-bi*) |

### Exceptions to Watch Out For:
- **Standalone On'yomi Nouns**: Numerals (一, 二, 三), calendar units (年, 月), and common spatial/technical nouns: **駅** (*eki* - station), **線** (*sen* - line), **本** (*hon* - book), **肉** (*niku* - meat), **席** (*seki* - seat), **点** (*ten* - point).
- **Kanji + する Verbs**: Verbs created by appending the auxiliary verb する to an On'yomi kanji noun: **愛する** (*ai-suru* - to love), **達する** (*tassuru* - to reach).

## 2. Hybrid Compounds: Mixed Readings (湯桶読み & 重箱読み)

On the JLPT N3 and N2 exams, questions frequently test **hybrid compounds** where a single word combines one On'yomi and one Kun'yomi.

### A. Yutō-yomi (湯桶読み) — [Kun'yomi] + [On'yomi]
Named after **湯桶 (ゆとう / *yu-tō*)** ("hot water pail"), combining native **湯 (ゆ / *yu*)** with Sino-Japanese **桶 (とう / *tō*)**:

| Hybrid Compound | Breakdown [Kun + On] | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **場所** (ばしょ / *ba-sho*) | **場** (*ba* - Kun) + **所** (*sho* - On) | Location / Place |
| **夕刊** (ゆうかん / *yū-kan*) | **夕** (*yū* - Kun) + **刊** (*kan* - On) | Evening newspaper |
| **荷物** (にもつ / *ni-motsu*) | **荷** (*ni* - Kun) + **物** (*motsu* - On) | Luggage / Package |
| **合図** (あいず / *ai-zu*) | **合** (*ai* - Kun) + **図** (*zu* - On) | Signal / Cue |
| **見本** (みほん / *mi-hon*) | **見** (*mi* - Kun) + **本** (*hon* - On) | Sample / Specimen |
| **役場** (やくば / *yaku-ba*) | **役** (*yaku* - On) + **場** (*ba* - Kun) | Town office |
| **手帳** (てちょう / *te-chō*) | **手** (*te* - Kun) + **帳** (*chō* - On) | Pocket notebook / Planner |

### B. Jūbako-yomi (重箱読み) — [On'yomi] + [Kun'yomi]
Named after **重箱 (じゅうばこ / *jū-bako*)** ("tiered food box"), combining Sino-Japanese **重 (じゅう / *jū*)** with native **箱 (ばこ / *bako*)**:

| Hybrid Compound | Breakdown [On + Kun] | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **番組** (ばんぐみ / *ban-gumi*) | **番** (*ban* - On) + **組** (*kumi/gumi* - Kun) | TV or radio show |
| **台所** (だいどころ / *dai-dokoro*) | **台** (*dai* - On) + **所** (*tokoro/dokoro* - Kun) | Kitchen |
| **毎朝** (まいあさ / *mai-asa*) | **毎** (*mai* - On) + **朝** (*asa* - Kun) | Every morning |
| **本棚** (ほんだな / *hon-dana*) | **本** (*hon* - On) + **棚** (*tana/dana* - Kun) | Bookshelf |
| **献立** (こんだて / *kon-date*) | **献** (*kon* - On) + **立** (*date* - Kun) | Menu / Meal plan |
| **桟橋** (さんばし / *san-bashi*) | **桟** (*san* - On) + **橋** (*hashi/bashi* - Kun) | Pier / Jetty |

## 3. Historical On'yomi Layers: Go-on, Kan-on, and Tō-on

Why does **生** have readings like **セイ**, **ショウ**, and **ジョウ**? Because Chinese pronunciation changed over centuries, and Japan imported characters in three successive historical waves:

```
5th–6th Century:  呉音 (Go-on)   ──> Southern Chinese dynasties; Buddhist & everyday terms
7th–9th Century:  漢音 (Kan-on)  ──> Tang Dynasty Chang'an; official government & scholarly standard
10th–14th Century: 唐音 (Tō-on)   ──> Song/Ming Dynasties; Zen Buddhism & specialized merchant imports
```

| Kanji | 呉音 (Go-on: Everyday / Buddhist) | 漢音 (Kan-on: Official / Modern Jukugo) | 唐音 (Tō-on: Specialized / Zen) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **行** | **ギョウ**: 行列 (*gyōretsu* - queue), 修行 (*shugyō*) | **コウ**: 銀行 (*ginkō* - bank), 旅行 (*ryokō*) | **アン**: 行灯 (*andon* - paper lantern) |
| **人** | **ニン**: 人間 (*ningen* - human), 三人 (*sannin*) | **ジン**: 日本人 (*Nihonjin*), 人口 (*jinkō*) | — |
| **日** | **ニチ**: 毎日 (*mainichi*), 日曜日 (*nichiyōbi*) | **ジツ**: 休日 (*kyūjitsu*), 本日 (*honjitsu*) | — |
| **明** | **ミョウ**: 明日 (*myōnichi*), 明白 (*myōhaku*) | **メイ**: 説明 (*setsumei*), 発明 (*hatsumei*) | **ミン**: 明朝体 (*minchōtai* - Mincho font) |
| **生** | **ショウ**: 一生 (*isshō* - lifetime) | **セイ**: 生活 (*seikatsu* - life), 学生 (*gakusei*) | — |

## 4. Semantic Shifts: Words Changing Meaning with Reading (N3–N2 Exam Trap)

Be cautious with compounds where switching between On'yomi and Kun'yomi creates entirely different words:

| Kanji Compound | Reading A (Pronunciation & Meaning) | Reading B (Pronunciation & Meaning) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **大人気** | **だいにんき** (*daininki*): Enormous popularity | **おとなげ** (*otonage*): Mature composure (大人げない = childish) |
| **生物** | **せいぶつ** (*seibutsu*): Living organism / Biology | **なまもの** (*namamono*): Raw food / Fresh perishables |
| **市場** | **しじょう** (*shijō*): Financial / Stock / Economic market | **いちば** (*ichiba*): Physical street / Wholesale fish market |
| **色紙** | **しきし** (*shikishi*): Square cardboard sign board for autographs | **いろがみ** (*irogami*): Colored origami / craft paper |
| **工夫** | **くふう** (*kufū*): Devising an ingenious method | **こうふ** (*kōfu*): Construction laborer / Miner |
| **黄金** | **おうごん** (*ōgon*): The golden era / Metaphorical gold | **こがね** (*kogane*): Literal golden bullion / raw gold metal |
