## 2. Near-Synonyms & Subtle Nuance Clusters (類義語 / Ruigigo)

Synonyms in Japanese often share an English translation but differ in **formality, probability, or domain of application**:

### A. Verbs for "To Change" (変える vs. 改める vs. 修正する vs. 更新する)
- **変える (かえる / *kaeru*)**: Generic, universally applicable ("to change/alter something").
  *例: 髪型を変える (change hairstyle).*
- **改める (あらためる / *aratameru*)**: Formal reform or moral/behavioral correction.
  *例: 態度を改める (correct one's attitude); 規則を改める (reform the rules).*
- **修正する (しゅうせいする / *shūsei suru*)**: Fixing specific errors or textual/technical discrepancies.
  *例: 原稿の誤字を修正する (correct typographical errors in a draft).*
- **更新する (こうしんする / *kōshin suru*)**: Extending validity, records, or software versions.
  *例: パスポートを更新する (renew passport); 世界記録を更新する (break a world record).*

### B. Adverbs of Immediacy (すぐ vs. 直ちに vs. たちまち)
- **すぐ (*sugu*)**: Everyday, standard conversational "right away" (subjective, within minutes).
  *例: すぐ行きます (I'll come right away).*
- **直ちに (ただちに / *tadachi ni*)**: Formal, emergency, zero-delay legal or administrative mandate.
  *例: 直ちに避難してください (Evacuate immediately without delay).*
- **たちまち (*tachimachi*)**: Narrative adverb meaning "in an instant / before one knew it" (focus on rapid transformation).
  *例: チケットはたちまち売り切れた (The tickets sold out in an instant).*

### C. Verbs of Answering (答える vs. 返事する vs. 応じる)
- **答える (こたえる / *kotaeru*)**: To provide a factual solution to a question or test problem.
  *例: 質問に答える (answer a question).*
- **返事する (へんじする / *henji suru*)**: To verbally acknowledge a call, greeting, or letter.
  *例: 名前を呼ばれて返事をする (reply when one's name is called).*
- **応じる (おうじる / *ōjiru*)**: To comply with, meet, or respond favorably to a request, demand, or invitation.
  *例: インタビューの依頼に応じる (agree/respond to an interview request).*

### D. Adverbs of Likelihood (たぶん vs. おそらく vs. もしかしたら)
- **たぶん (*tabun*)**: ~70% colloquial probability ("probably / I think so").
- **おそらく (*osoraku*)**: ~85% formal objective probability ("in all likelihood / highly probable").
- **もしかしたら (*moshikashitara*)**: ~30% low possibility ("perhaps / by some slim chance").
