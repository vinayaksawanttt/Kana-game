# JLPT N1 Grammar Patterns: Master Reference Sheet

The **JLPT N1** level represents native-level mastery of literary, academic, legal, and oratorical Japanese. It heavily incorporates **classical Japanese (古語 / *kogo*) remnants**, **dramatic rhetoric**, **profound emotional outbursts**, and **hyper-precise chronological sequences**.

---

## 1. Classical & Literary Verb Forms

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Nuance & Function | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜であれ / 〜であろうと** | Noun + であれ / であろうと | *Be it [A] or [B] / Regardless of what/who (unconditional)* | たとえ国王**であれ**、法に従わなければならない。<br/>(*Even were one a king, one must obey the law.*) |
| **〜んばかりに / 〜んばかりだ** | Verb [ない-stem] + んばかりに<br/>(する → せんばかり) | *As if on the very verge of doing / As though about to...* | 彼は今にも泣き出さ**んばかりの**表情をした。<br/>(*He wore an expression as if on the very verge of bursting into tears.*) |
| **〜まじき** | Verb [dict.] + まじき + Noun<br/>(する → すまじき) | *Unforgivable / Must never do [from a professional/moral status]* | 弱者を侮辱するなど、教育者としてある**まじき**行為だ。<br/>(*Insulting the weak is an act unbefitting of an educator.*) |
| **〜べく** | Verb [dict.] + べく<br/>(する → すべく / するべく) | *In order to / With the firm intention of doing (classical ために)* | 新たな市場を開拓す**べく**、海外へ視察団を派遣した。<br/>(*In order to pioneer new markets, an inspection mission was dispatched abroad.*) |
| **〜ずにはおかない / 〜ないではおかない** | Verb [ない-stem] + ずにはおかない | *Will inevitably cause / Cannot fail to bring about* | この感動的な小説は、読む者の涙を誘わ**ずにはおかない**。<br/>(*This moving novel will not fail to move its readers to tears.*) |

## 2. Intense Emotional States & Overwhelming Feelings

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Meaning & Register | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜を禁じ得ない (をきんじえない)** | Noun (emotion) + を禁じ得ない | *Cannot suppress / Cannot help feeling [deep sorrow, anger, admiration]* | 不正を平然と行う指導者に、強い怒り**を禁じ得ない**。<br/>(*I cannot suppress intense indignation at a leader who brazenly commits fraud.*) |
| **〜にたえない** | Noun / Verb [dict.] + にたえない | 1. *Cannot bear to witness (pitiful/grotesque)*<br/>2. *Overcome with deep [gratitude, joy]* | 悲惨な事故現場は、見る**にたえない**光景だった。<br/>(*The tragic accident scene was a sight too painful to bear watching.*)<br/>皆様のご支援には感謝**にたえません**。<br/>(*I am filled with gratitude beyond words for all your support.*) |
| **〜極まりない / 〜極まる** | な-Adj stem / い-Adj + 極まりない | *Extremely / To the absolute limit of [danger, rudeness, regret]* | 命綱なしで高所に登るのは、危険**極まりない**。<br/>(*Climbing high places without a safety rope is reckless in the extreme.*) |
| **〜てやまない** | Verb [て-form] + やまない | *Ceaselessly / From the very bottom of my heart (wishing, praying)* | 被災地の一日も早い復興を祈っ**てやみません**。<br/>(*I pray ceaselessly from the bottom of my heart for the swift recovery of the disaster area.*) |

## 3. Strict Preconditions & Absolute Inevitability

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜なしには / 〜なくしては (…ない)** | Noun + なしには / なくしては | *Without [A], [B] is completely impossible to achieve* | 市民の協力**なしには**、この計画の成功はあり得ない。<br/>(*Without the citizens' cooperation, the success of this plan is impossible.*) |
| **〜を余儀なくされる (をよぎなくされる)** | Noun + を余儀なくされる | *Be forced into / Compelled to undergo an undesirable situation* | 台風の接近により、野外フェスの延期**を余儀なくされた**。<br/>(*Due to the approaching typhoon, the outdoor festival was forced to be postponed.*) |
| **〜ないまでも** | Verb [ない-form] + までも | *Even if it doesn't achieve [ideal A], at least [realistic B]* | 満点とはいか**ないまでも**、合格点は取りたい。<br/>(*Even if I don't get a perfect score, I at least want to achieve a passing grade.*) |
| **〜を皮切りに(して) (をかわきりに)** | Noun + を皮切りに | *Starting off with [A], a succession of events unfolds* | 東京公演**を皮切りに**、全国5大都市ツアーが始まった。<br/>(*Starting with the Tokyo show, the five-city nationwide tour kicked off.*) |

## 4. Immediate Succession: "The Very Instant..."

| Pattern | Formation | Nuance | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜や否や (やいなや)** | Verb [dict.] + や否や | *The moment [A happened], instantly [B occurred]* | ベルが鳴る**や否や**、生徒たちは教室を飛び出した。<br/>(*The moment the bell rang, the pupils bolted out of the classroom.*) |
| **〜が早いか** | Verb [dict. / た] + が早いか | *No sooner had [A happened] than [instant spontaneous B]* | 終業のチャイムが鳴る**が早いか**、席を立った。<br/>(*No sooner had the chime sounded than he stood from his seat.*) |
| **〜そばから** | Verb [dict. / た] + そばから | *No sooner does one do [A] than [repeated futile cycle of B]* | 新しい単語を覚える**そばから**、忘れていく。<br/>(*No sooner do I memorize new vocabulary than I forget it.*) |
| **〜なり** | Verb [dict.] + なり | *Immediately upon doing [A], an unexpected action B was taken* | 彼は手紙を読む**なり**、顔色を変えて部屋を出て行った。<br/>(*The moment he read the letter, his face changed color and he left the room.*) |

## 5. Defiance, Interplay & Perspective

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜をものともせずに** | Noun + をものともせずに | *In complete defiance of / Undaunted by [hardship, danger, storm]* | 隊員たちは吹き荒れる猛吹雪**をものともせずに**前進した。<br/>(*The crew pressed forward undaunted by the raging blizzard.*) |
| **〜をよそに** | Noun + をよそに | *Indifferent to / Paying no heed to [other people's worry, criticism]* | 親の心配**をよそに**、彼は一人で海外へ旅立った。<br/>(*Paying no heed to his parents' worries, he set off traveling abroad alone.*) |
| **〜と相まって (とあいまって)** | Noun + と相まって | *Coupled with / Interacting mutually with [A and B combine]* | 美しい映像が素晴らしい音楽**と相まって**、深い感動を与えた。<br/>(*The gorgeous visuals coupled with the magnificent music produced profound emotion.*) |
| **〜に言わせれば** | Noun + に言わせれば | *If you ask [expert/critic] / From the perspective of...* | 専門家**に言わせれば**、この修復作業は初歩的なミスだ。<br/>(*If you ask the experts, this restoration work was an elementary mistake.*) |
