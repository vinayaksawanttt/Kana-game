# JLPT N4 Kanji Complete Reference Sheet (181 Kanji)

Building directly upon the 103 N5 kanji, the **JLPT N4** level adds **181 kanji**, bringing your cumulative inventory to ~284 characters. At this stage, mastery shifts from standalone pictographs to **multi-kanji compounds (熟語 / *jukugo*)**, transitive/intransitive verb pairings, and daily life communicative vocabulary.
