# JLPT N2 & N1 Kanji Master Reference Guide

At the advanced levels of the Japanese Language Proficiency Test:
- **JLPT N2 (~370 new kanji; ~1,000 cumulative)** completes all 1,026 elementary **Kyōiku Kanji (教育漢字)** and incorporates junior-high characters essential for business correspondence, newspaper editorials, and formal notifications.
- **JLPT N1 (~1,130 new kanji; ~2,136 cumulative)** spans the entire **Jōyō Kanji (常用漢字)** catalog, incorporating academic treatises, legal codes, medical literature, political analysis, and classical/literary Kun'yomi verbs.
