# JLPT N3 Kanji Reference Sheet (~370 Kanji)

The **JLPT N3** bridges the elementary foundations (N5–N4) and full functional literacy, adding approximately **370 new kanji** to reach a cumulative total of ~650 characters.

At this level, kanji usage shifts heavily from physical objects toward **abstract relationships, internal psychological states, institutional vocabulary, news media, and cognitive verbs**.
