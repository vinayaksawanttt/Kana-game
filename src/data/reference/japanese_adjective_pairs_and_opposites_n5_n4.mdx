# Japanese Adjective Pairs and Opposites (対義語)

Studying adjectives in **antonym pairs (対義語 / *taigigo*)** accelerates memorization and deepens conceptual understanding. Knowing that **高い** (*takai*) contrasts with both **低い** (*hikui*, height) and **安い** (*yasui*, price) gives an immediate mental map of the vocabulary.

Below are the most critical JLPT N5 and N4 adjective pairs, organized by semantic domain.

---

## 1. Physical Dimensions, Size, and Quantity

| Word A (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Word B (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **大きい** (*ōkii*) | big / large | **小さい** (*chiisai*) | small / little | N5 |
| **長い** (*nagai*) | long | **短い** (*mijikai*) | short (length) | N5 |
| **高い** (*takai*) | high / tall | **低い** (*hikui*) | low / short (stature) | N5 |
| **太い** (*futoi*) | thick / stout / fat | **細い** (*hosoi*) | thin / slender / fine | N5 |
| **広い** (*hiroi*) | spacious / wide | **狭い** (*semai*) | narrow / cramped | N5 |
| **深い** (*fukai*) | deep | **浅い** (*asai*) | shallow | N4 |
| **重い** (*omoi*) | heavy | **軽い** (*karui*) | light (weight) | N5 |
| **多い** (*ōi*) | many / abundant | **少ない** (*sukunai*) | few / scarce | N5 |

> [!NOTE]
> **多い** and **少ない** are rarely used directly before a noun (e.g., avoid \*多い人). Instead, use them as predicates: **人が多い** (*Hito ga ōi*, "People are many" / "There are many people") or use adverbs like **たくさん** (*takusan*).

## 2. Evaluation, Quality, Price, and Difficulty

| Word A (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Word B (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **良い / いい** (*ii / yoi*) | good | **悪い** (*warui*) | bad | N5 |
| **高い** (*takai*) | expensive | **安い** (*yasui*) | cheap / inexpensive | N5 |
| **新しい** (*atarashii*) | new / fresh | **古い** (*furui*) | old (objects) | N5 |
| **難しい** (*muzukashii*) | difficult / hard | **易しい** (*yasashii*) | easy / simple | N4 |
| **簡単な** (*kantan na*) | simple / effortless | **複雑な** (*fukuzatsu na*) | complex / complicated | N5/N4 |
| **正しい** (*tadashii*) | correct / righteous | **間違っている** (*machigatte iru*) | mistaken / wrong | N4 |

> [!WARNING]
> **古い** (*furui*) is used exclusively for **objects, buildings, and ideas**, never for human beings. For an older person, use **年上** (*toshiue*, older in age) or **お年寄り** (*otoshiyori*, elderly person).

## 4. Climate, Temperature, Light, and Touch

Japanese differentiates meteorological climate, liquid/object temperature, and emotional warmth through distinct kanji and vocabulary:

| Word A (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Word B (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **暑い** (*atsui*) | hot (ambient weather) | **寒い** (*samui*) | cold (ambient weather) | N5 |
| **熱い** (*atsui*) | hot (object, liquid, touch) | **冷たい** (*tsumetai*) | cold (object, liquid, touch) | N5 |
| **暖かい** (*atatakai*) | pleasantly warm (weather) | **涼しい** (*suzushii*) | pleasantly cool (weather) | N4 |
| **温かい** (*atatakai*) | warm (food, tea, soup) | **冷たい** (*tsumetai*) | cold (drink, touch, heart) | N4 |
| **明るい** (*akarui*) | bright / well-lit | **暗い** (*kurai*) | dark / dim | N5 |
| **強い** (*tsuyoi*) | strong / powerful | **弱い** (*yowai*) | weak / fragile | N5 |
| **硬い / 固い** (*katai*) | hard / firm / solid | **柔らかい** (*yawarakai*) | soft / tender | N4 |

### The Critical Temperature Distinctions

```
Weather & Atmosphere:     暑い (hot weather)  ↔  寒い (cold weather)
Objects & Liquids:        熱い (hot soup)     ↔  冷たい (cold water)
Mild / Pleasant Season:   暖かい (warm spring) ↔  涼しい (cool autumn)
```

- **今日は暑いですね。** (*Kyō wa atsui desu ne.* — It is hot today, isn't it? [Atmospheric])
- **このお茶は熱いです。** (*Kono ocha wa atsui desu.* — This green tea is hot. [Liquid])
- **風が冷たいです。** (*Kaze ga tsumetai desu.* — The wind is freezing cold against the skin.)

## 6. Core な-Adjective Antonyms

な-Adjectives include many pairs constructed with prefix negation (such as **不- / 無-**) or conceptual polarity:

| Word A (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Word B (Kanji & Romaji) | Meaning | Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **好きな** (*suki na*) | liked / favorite | **嫌いな** (*kirai na*) | disliked / hated | N5 |
| **上手な** (*jōzu na*) | skillful (objective) | **下手な** (*heta na*) | unskillful / clumsy | N5 |
| **得意な** (*tokui na*) | strong at / confident in | **苦手な** (*nigate na*) | weak at / uncomfortable with | N4 |
| **静かな** (*shizuka na*) | quiet / peaceful | **賑やかな** (*nigiyaka na*) | lively / bustling | N5 |
| **便利な** (*benri na*) | convenient / handy | **不便な** (*fuben na*) | inconvenient | N5 |
| **親切な** (*shinsetsu na*) | kind / accommodating | **不親切な** (*fushinsetsu na*) | unkind / unhelpful | N5/N4 |
| **元気な** (*genki na*) | energetic / in good health | **病気の** (*byōki no*) | sick / ill | N5 |
| **安全な** (*anzen na*) | safe / secure | **危険な** (*kiken na*) | dangerous / hazardous | N4 |
| **有名な** (*yūmei na*) | famous / well-known | **無名な** (*mumei na*) | unknown / obscure | N5/N4 |
| **必要な** (*hitsuyō na*) | necessary / needed | **不要な** (*fuyō na*) | unnecessary / unneeded | N4 |
| **真面目な** (*majime na*) | diligent / serious | **不真面目な** (*fumajime na*) | insincere / frivolous | N4 |

### 上手/下手 vs. 得意/苦手
- **上手 / 下手** evaluates objective skill or performance (often used for others):
  - 田中さんはピアノが**上手**です。 (*Mr. Tanaka is good at the piano.*)
- **得意 / 苦手** expresses personal confidence, comfort, or subjective aptitude:
  - 私は数学が**苦手**です。 (*I am not confident with math.*)
  - Modesty convention: Avoid describing yourself as 上手 (*jōzu*); use **得意** (*tokui*) instead.
