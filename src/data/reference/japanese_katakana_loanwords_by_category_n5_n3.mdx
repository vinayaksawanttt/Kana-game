# Katakana Loanwords by Category & Wasei-Eigo (外来語・和製英語)

Loanwords (**外来語 / *gairaigo*)** form roughly **10% of modern Japanese vocabulary**. While the vast majority derive from English, historical trade introduced foundational terms from **Portuguese**, **Dutch**, **German**, and **French**.

Furthermore, Japanese speakers frequently invent **Wasei-Eigo (和製英語 — Japanese-Made English)**: pseudo-English coinages whose meanings differ drastically from standard English.

## 6. Wasei-Eigo (和製英語) Traps: Words That Mislead English Speakers!

| Wasei-Eigo Term | Literal English | What It Actually Means in Japan | True English Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **マンション (*manshon*)** | Mansion | Standard multi-family concrete apartment | Apartment / Condominium |
| **コンセント (*konsento*)** | Concentric | The wall electrical receptacle | Power socket / Wall outlet |
| **クラクション (*kurakushon*)** | Klaxon (brand) | Automobile warning horn | Car horn |
| **スキンシップ (*sukinshippu*)** | Skin-ship | Physical affection / bonding with babies/partners | Cuddling / Physical intimacy |
| **カンニング (*kanningu*)** | Cunning | Illicit cheating during an examination | Cheating on a test |
| **サラリーマン (*sararīman*)** | Salary-man | Regular male office employee | White-collar office worker |
| **チャージ (*chāji*)** | Charge | Loading electronic money onto Suica/Pasmo cards | To reload / Top up an IC card |
| **プラス / マイナス ドライバー** | Plus/Minus driver| Screwdrivers based on head shape | Phillips (+) / Flathead (-) screwdriver |
| **オーダーメイド (*ōdāmeido*)** | Order-made | Custom goods built to customer specification | Custom-made / Bespoke |

## 7. Clipped Katakana Abbreviations (略語 / Ryakugo)

Japanese speakers routinely abbreviate 4+ mora English phrases into snappy 4-mora units:

```
  リモート・コントローラー  ──>  リモコン (rimokon - Remote Control)
  エアコンディショナー      ──>  エアコン (eakon - Air Conditioner)
  コンビニエンス・ストア    ──>  コンビニ (konbini - Convenience Store)
  デジタル・カメラ          ──>  デジカメ (dejikame - Digital Camera)
  アニメーション            ──>  アニメ (anime - Animation)
  プロフェッショナル        ──>  プロ (puro - Professional)
```
