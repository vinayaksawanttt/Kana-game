# Japanese Directions & Location Words (位置・方角)

Accurately navigating cities, finding misplaced objects, and describing physical layouts in Japanese requires mastering two complementary vocabularies:
1. **Cardinal Directions (方角 / *hōgaku*)**: North, South, East, West, and train station exits.
2. **Relative Spatial Positions (位置 / *ichi*)**: Top, bottom, front, back, adjacent, and between.

---

## 1. Cardinal Directions (東西南北 / Tōzai Nanboku)

In Japanese, the four compass directions are traditionally listed in the compound order **東・西・南・北 (とうざいなんぼく / *tōzai nanboku* — East, West, South, North)**:

| Direction | Kanji | Reading | Romaji | Real-World Station / Geography Examples |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **North** | **北** | きた | *kita* | **北海道** (*Hokkaidō*), **北口** (*kitaguchi* - north exit) |
| **South** | **南** | みなみ | *minami* | **東南アジア** (*Tōnan Ajia* - SE Asia), **南口** (*minamiguchi*) |
| **East** | **東** | ひがし | *higashi* | **東京** (*Tōkyō* - Eastern Capital), **東口** (*higashiguchi*) |
| **West** | **西** | にし | *nishi* | **西武線** (*Seibu-sen*), **西口** (*nishiguchi* - west exit) |

### Intermediate Quadrants:
- **Northeast**: **北東** (ほくとう / *hokutō*)
- **Southeast**: **南東** (なんとう / *nantō*)
- **Southwest**: **南西** (なんせい / *nansei*)
- **Northwest**: **北西** (ほくせい / *hokusei*)
- **Center**: **中央** (ちゅうおう / *chūō*) $\rightarrow$ **中央口** (*chūōguchi* - central gate)

## 2. Relative Spatial Relationship Nouns (位置を表す名詞)

In Japanese, spatial prepositions (like "on", "under", "inside") are grammatically **nouns**. They must be joined to reference points using the particle **の (*no*)**:

| Position | Kanji | Reading | Romaji | Meaning | Sample Phrase |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Above / On** | **上** | うえ | *ue* | On top of / Above | 机の**上** (*on top of the desk*) |
| **Below / Under**| **下** | した | *shita* | Below / Underneath | 木の**下** (*under the tree*) |
| **Front** | **前** | まえ | *mae* | In front of / Before | 駅の**前** (*in front of the station*) |
| **Back / Behind**| **後ろ** | うしろ | *ushiro* | Behind / In the back | ドアの**後ろ** (*behind the door*) |
| **Right** | **右** | みぎ | *migi* | Right side | 車の**右** (*to the right of the car*) |
| **Left** | **左** | ひだり | *hidari* | Left side | 画面の**左** (*to the left of the screen*) |
| **Inside** | **中** | なか | *naka* | Inside / In | 箱の**中** (*inside the box*) |
| **Outside** | **外** | そと | *soto* | Outside | 家の**外** (*outside the house*) |
| **Next door** | **隣** | となり | *tonari* | Adjacent / Next to | 本屋の**隣** (*next to the bookstore*) |
| **Beside / Side** | **横** | よこ | *yoko* | Beside / Horizontal side | 机の**横** (*beside the desk*) |
| **Between** | **間** | あいだ | *aida* | Between (two items) | AとBの**間** (*between A and B*) |
| **Nearby** | **近く** | ちかく | *chikaku* | In the vicinity of | 病院の**近く** (*near the hospital*) |
| **Across from** | **向かい** | むかい | *mukai* | Directly opposite | 郵便局の**向かい** (*across from the post office*) |

## 3. The Spatial Sentence Formula

To state where something is located, combine the location noun with the existence particle **に (*ni*)** and the existence verbs **ある (*aru* — for inanimate objects)** or **いる (*iru* — for living beings)**:

```
[Reference Entity] の [Location Noun] に [Subject] が あります / います
```

### Examples:
1. **机の上に本があります。**  
   *Tsukue no ue ni hon ga arimasu.*  
   (There is a book on top of the desk.)
2. **椅子の下に猫がいます。**  
   *Isu no shita ni neko ga imasu.*  
   (There is a cat under the chair.)
3. **銀行と郵便局の間にカフェがあります。**  
   *Ginkō to yūbinkyoku no aida ni kafe ga arimasu.*  
   (There is a cafe between the bank and the post office.)
4. **田中さんはコンビニの前にいます。**  
   *Tanaka-san wa konbini no mae ni imasu.*  
   (Mr. Tanaka is in front of the convenience store.)

## 4. Nuance Breakdown: 隣 (*Tonari*) vs. 横 (*Yoko*) vs. そば (*Soba*)

A common question on JLPT N5 and N4 is deciding which "next to" word to use:

| Word | Specific Nuance | When to Use | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **隣 (となり / *tonari*)** | **Immediate neighbor / Adjacent** (Usually of the same conceptual category, with nothing between) | People sitting side-by-side, neighboring houses, adjacent stores. | 田中さんの隣 (*sitting next to Tanaka*); 私の家の隣 (*the house next door*) |
| **横 (よこ / *yoko*)** | **Horizontal flank / Side** (Can be anything on your left or right, even if completely different categories) | Any object placed to the left or right of a reference point. | ゴミ箱の横に傘がある (*There is an umbrella to the side of the trash can*) |
| **そば (*soba*)** | **Near proximity / Vicinity** (Not strictly on the horizontal axis; close by) | Close proximity without requiring immediate adjacency. | 私のそばにいて (*Stay close beside me*) |

## 5. Giving & Following Street Directions (道案内)

Essential verbs and directional phrases used when navigating Japanese towns:

- **まっすぐ行く** (*massugu iku*): To go straight ahead.
- **右に曲がる** (*migi ni magaru*): To turn right.
- **左に曲がる** (*hidari ni magaru*): To turn left.
- **道を渡る** (*michi o wataru*): To cross the street.
- **橋を渡る** (*hashi o wataru*): To cross the bridge.
- **一つ目の角** (*hitotsu-me no kado*): The first corner.
- **二つ目の信号** (*futatsu-me no shingō*): The second traffic light.
- **突き当たり** (*tsukiatari*): The end of the street / T-junction (where the road dead-ends).
