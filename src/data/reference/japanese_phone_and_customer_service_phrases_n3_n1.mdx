# Japanese Phone and Customer Service Phrases (電話対応・接客用語)

Handling telephone calls and serving customers in Japan requires strict conformity to professional etiquette. Because callers and customers cannot see your face, vocal tone, speed, clarity, and precise keigo carry the entire weight of the organization's reputation.

---

## 1. Professional Telephone Protocol (ビジネス電話対応)

In Japanese offices, the telephone should ideally be answered within **two rings**. If it rings three times or more, standard etiquette requires opening with an apology for the delay: **「大変お待たせいたしました。」**

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               Standard 5-Step Phone Flow                │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Answer:    お電話ありがとうございます。               │
│               株式会社〇〇の[姓]でございます。         │
│ 2. Acknowledge: 〇〇様でいらっしゃいますね。           │
│               いつも大変お世話になっております。       │
│ 3. Transfer:  営業部の佐藤でございますね。             │
│               ただいまおつなぎいたします。             │
│ 4. Take Msg:  あいにく佐藤は外出しております。         │
│               よろしければご伝言を承りましょうか。     │
│ 5. Hang Up:   お電話ありがとうございました。           │
│               失礼いたします。(Wait for client to click) │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Phone Call Phrases by Scenario

### A. Answering and Identifying Callers

| Scenario | Japanese Phrase | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Answering promptly** | **お電話ありがとうございます。株式会社ABCの山田でございます。** | Thank you for calling. This is Yamada from ABC Corporation. |
| **Answering after 3+ rings** | **大変お待たせいたしました。株式会社ABCの山田でございます。** | Thank you very much for waiting. This is Yamada from ABC Corporation. |
| **Confirming caller identity** | **〇〇会社の田中様でいらっしゃいますね。いつもお世話になっております。** | That would be Mr. Tanaka of OO Company, correct? Thank you as always for your support. |
| **Didn't catch caller's name** | **恐れ入りますが、お電話が少々遠いようでございます。もう一度お名前を伺えますでしょうか。** | Excuse me, but the connection seems a bit faint. Could you please repeat your name? |

---

### B. Putting on Hold and Transferring Calls

| Scenario | Japanese Phrase | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Putting on hold** | **確認いたしますので、少々お待ちくださいませ。** | I will check that for you; please hold for a moment. |
| **Transferring to colleague** | **担当の佐藤におつなぎいたします。少々お待ちいただけますでしょうか。** | I will connect you to Sato in charge. Could you please hold for a moment? |
| **Resuming after hold** | **お待たせいたしました。担当の佐藤に代わります。** | Thank you for waiting. I am now transferring you to Sato. |

---

### C. When the Target Person is Unavailable

When the requested colleague cannot take the call, state the reason politely **without using honorifics for your own colleague**:

| Status | Spoken Reason to Caller |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Stepped away from desk** | **あいにく佐藤は席を外しております。** (*Ainiku Sato wa seki o hazushite orimasu.*) |
| **In a meeting** | **ただいま佐藤は別の会議（ミーティング）に入っております。** (*...kaigi ni haitte orimasu.*) |
| **On another phone call** | **申し訳ございません。佐藤は別の電話に出ております。** (*...betsu no denwa ni dete orimasu.*) |
| **Out of the office** | **佐藤はただいま外出しておりまして、17時頃戻る予定でございます。** (*...gaishutsu shite orimashite...*) |
| **Day off / Vacation** | **佐藤は本日、休暇（休み）をいただいております。** (*...kyūka o itadaite orimasu.*) |

---

### D. Offering a Callback or Taking a Message

- **戻り次第、佐藤から折り返しお電話を差し上げましょうか。**
  *(Modorishidai, Sato kara orikaeshi o-denwa o sashiagemashō ka.)*
  — Shall I have Sato call you back as soon as they return?
- **私、山田がご伝言を承りましょうか。**
  *(Watakushi, Yamada ga go-dengon o uketamawarima shō ka.)*
  — Would you like me, Yamada, to take a message?
- **念のため、お電話番号とお名前を復唱させていただきます。**
  *(Nen no tame, o-denwabangō to o-namae o fukushō sasete itadakimasu.)*
  — Just to be sure, allow me to repeat your phone number and name.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Phone Hanging-Up Rule (受話器を置く作法)**:
> In Japanese business etiquette, **the client or superior must hang up first**. Always wait until you hear the line disconnect before gently pressing down the receiver hook with your finger. Slamming the handset down onto the cradle is considered rude.

## 3. Customer Service: The 7 Golden Phrases (接客の基本7大用語)

In Japanese retail, hospitality, dining, and airlines, all staff are trained in the **接客基本7大用語 (*Setsu-kyaku Kihon Nana-dai Yōgo*)**:

| # | Japanese Phrase | Romaji | Meaning & Usage |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **いらっしゃいませ** | *Irasshaimase* | Welcome! (Entering a shop/hotel/restaurant). |
| **2** | **かしこまりました** | *Kashikomarimashita* | Certainly / Understood (taking an order or customer request). |
| **3** | **少々お待ちくださいませ** | *Shōshō o-machi kudasai mase* | Please wait a brief moment. |
| **4** | **大変お待たせいたしました** | *Taihen o-matase itashimashita* | Thank you very much for waiting (bringing food, returning with items). |
| **5** | **恐れ入ります** | *Osoreirimasu* | Excuse me / Pardon me (prefacing a question or receiving a favor). |
| **6** | **申し訳ございません** | *Mōshiwake gozaimasen* | We deeply apologize (errors, out of stock, delays). |
| **7** | **ありがとうございました。またお越しくださいませ** | *Arigatō gozaimashita. Mata okoshi kudasai mase* | Thank you very much. Please visit us again! (Customer departing). |

## 4. Cash Register Etiquette & "Baito-Keigo" (レジ対応とバイト敬語)

Handling money at checkout requires precision. Untrained retail staff often slip into **バイト敬語 (*Baito-keigo* / Part-time Keigo)**, which is widely criticized for grammatical inaccuracy.

```
Common Cashier Transactions:
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Customer hands over money:                             │
│ ❌ 10,000円からお預かりします。 (Ungrammatical "kara") │
│ ⭕ 10,000円をお預かりいたします。                      │
│                                                        │
│ Exact change handed:                                   │
│ ⭕ ちょうどいただきます。(I accept the exact amount)   │
│                                                        │
│ Giving change back:                                    │
│ ⭕ 3,000円のお返しと、レシートでございます。            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Top 3 Baito-Keigo Mistakes Corrected

| Grammatically Flawed (*Baito-keigo*) | Why it's wrong | Grammatically Correct (*Tadashii Keigo*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ご注文は以上でよろしかったでしょうか？** | Uses unnatural past tense (*deshita*) for an immediate present choice. | **ご注文は以上でよろしいでしょうか。** (*...yoroshii deshō ka.*) |
| **こちら、ホットコーヒーになります。** | The coffee is not "transforming" (*naru*) into coffee; it already is coffee! | **こちら、ホットコーヒーでございます。** (*...de gozaimasu.*) |
| **5000円からお預かりします。** | The particle **から** implies "from 5,000 yen I take change", which is incorrect grammar. | **5000円をお預かりいたします。** (*...o o-azukari itashimasu.*) |
