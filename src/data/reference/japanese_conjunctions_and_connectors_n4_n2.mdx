# Japanese Conjunctions and Discourse Connectors (接続詞)

In Japanese, **conjunctions (接続詞 / *setsuzokushi*)** link clauses, sentences, and entire paragraphs. For intermediate and advanced learners (JLPT N4–N2), mastering connectors is the single most important skill for **reading comprehension (読解 / *dokkai*)** and coherent writing.

Connectors fall into five major logical categories based on the relationship they establish between the preceding clause (**A**) and the following clause (**B**).

---

## Master Category Overview

| Category | Logical Relationship | Core Question Answered | Key Connectors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Contrast & Concession (逆接)** | A and B oppose each other or defy expectations | "However...", "In spite of that..." | しかし, でも, ところが, それなのに, 一方で |
| **2. Cause & Consequence (順接)** | A naturally produces or leads to B | "Therefore...", "As a result..." | だから, そのため, したがって, そこで, すると |
| **3. Addition & Accumulation (累加・並立)** | B adds extra information on top of A | "Moreover...", "Furthermore...", "And..." | そして, それから, さらに, その上, しかも, また |
| **4. Explanation & Summary (換言・説明)** | B rephrases, summarizes, or explains A | "In other words...", "Namely...", "Because..." | つまり, すなわち, 要するに, なぜなら |
| **5. Topic Shift (転換)** | Leaves topic A to introduce new topic B | "By the way...", "Well now..." | さて, ところで, では / それでは |

## 1. Contrast and Concession (逆接 / Gyakusetsu)

Used when clause B contradicts, limits, or defies the expectations set by clause A:

| Connector | Register / Level | Nuance | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **しかし** (*shikashi*) | Formal / N4–N3 | Standard "However"; marks a serious, objective contrast | 努力した。**しかし**、結果は出なかった。<br/>(*I made an effort. However, results did not follow.*) |
| **でも** (*demo*) | Spoken / N4 | Casual "But"; conversational | 行きたい。**でも**、お金がない。<br/>(*I want to go. But I have no money.*) |
| **ところが** (*tokoroga*) | Narrative / N3 | "To my surprise / Contrary to expectations..." (factual surprise) | 晴れると思った。**ところが**、大雨になった。<br/>(*I thought it would clear up. Contrary to expectations, it poured.*) |
| **それなのに** (*sorenanoni*) | Emotional / N3 | "And yet / In spite of that!" (expresses dissatisfaction/disbelief) | 一生懸命勉強した。**それなのに**、不合格だった。<br/>(*I studied with all my might. And yet, I failed!*) |
| **一方(で)** (*ippō de*) | Analytical / N3–N2 | "On the one hand... on the other hand..." (contrasting two aspects) | 都会は便利だ。**一方で**、生活費が高い。<br/>(*Big cities are convenient. On the other hand, living costs are high.*) |
| **にもかかわらず** (*ni mo kakawarazu*) | Formal / N2 | "Nevertheless / Despite this..." (formal contrast) | 悪天候**にもかかわらず**、多くの客が集まった。<br/>(*Despite the foul weather, a large crowd gathered.*) |

## 2. Cause and Consequence (順接 / Junsetsu)

Used when clause A is the reason, premise, or trigger that leads to clause B:

| Connector | Register / Level | Precise Functional Role | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **だから** (*dakara*) | Informal / N4 | Spoken "So / Therefore" | 雨が降っている。**だから**、傘を持っていく。<br/>(*It's raining. So, I will take an umbrella.*) |
| **そのため** (*sono tame*) | Formal / N3 | Factual "For that reason / Consequently" (cause-effect without personal bias) | 事故があった。**そのため**、電車が遅れている。<br/>(*There was an accident. Consequently, trains are delayed.*) |
| **したがって** (*shitagatte*) | Academic / N2 | Logical "Therefore / In conclusion" (formal essays, reports) | AとBは等しい。**したがって**、Cも等しい。<br/>(*A equals B. Therefore, C is also equal.*) |
| **それで** (*sorede*) | Narrative / N4 | "And so / Because of that..." (chronological result) | 夜更かしをした。**それで**、今朝寝坊した。<br/>(*I stayed up late. And so, I overslept this morning.*) |
| **そこで** (*sokode*) | Action-oriented / N3 | "In light of that, I took action..." (speaker's deliberate response) | 会場が狭かった。**そこで**、広い部屋に変更した。<br/>(*The venue was too cramped. Thereupon, we moved to a larger room.*) |
| **すると** (*suruto*) | Automatic / N3 | "Upon doing that, immediately..." (natural consequence or discovery) | 窓を開けた。**すると**、冷たい風が入ってきた。<br/>(*I opened the window. Thereupon, cold wind rushed in.*) |

### The Critical Trio: だから vs. それで vs. そこで
- **だから**: Emphasizes the **subjective judgment or conclusion** ("So naturally...").
- **それで**: Emphasizes the **factual consequence** ("And that's why it turned out that way").
- **そこで**: Emphasizes a **deliberate decision or action taken to solve a situation** ("Faced with that, I took this step").

## 3. Addition and Cumulative Expansion (累加・並立)

Used to add extra facts, arguments, or layers of meaning:

| Connector | Level | Nuance & Function | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **そして** (*soshite*) | N5–N4 | Neutral "And / Then" (sequence or addition) | 顔を洗った。**そして**、朝食を食べた。<br/>(*I washed my face. And then, I ate breakfast.*) |
| **それから** (*sorekara*) | N5–N4 | Chronological "And after that..." | 宿題をした。**それから**、テレビを見た。<br/>(*I did my homework. After that, I watched TV.*) |
| **その上** (*sono ue*) | N3 | "On top of that / Moreover" (reinforces tone) | 彼は頭がいい。**その上**、スポーツも万能だ。<br/>(*He is smart. On top of that, he is an all-around athlete.*) |
| **さらに** (*sara ni*) | N3–N2 | "Furthermore / Even more" (formal escalation) | 物価が上がり、**さらに**税金も増えた。<br/>(*Prices rose, and furthermore taxes increased.*) |
| **しかも** (*shikamo*) | N3 | "What is more / And to boot" (adds a decisive extra punch) | この靴は軽くて丈夫だ。**しかも**、値段が安い。<br/>(*These shoes are light and sturdy. What's more, they are cheap!*) |
| **また** (*mata*) | N4–N3 | "Also / In addition" (written coordination) | 英語を教えている。**また**、翻訳もしている。<br/>(*I teach English. In addition, I also do translation.*) |

## 4. Explanation, Reformulation & Summary (換言・説明)

Vital for following the author's core thesis in JLPT reading passages:

| Connector | Level | Role | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **つまり** (*tsumari*) | N3 | "In other words / In short" (restates the core point simply) | 来週の月曜日は祝日だ。**つまり**、学校は休みだ。<br/>(*Next Monday is a national holiday. In other words, school is off.*) |
| **すなわち** (*sunawachi*) | N2 | "Namely / That is to say" (formal, precise equivalence) | 日本の首都、**すなわち**東京で開かれる。<br/>(*It will be held in the capital of Japan, namely Tokyo.*) |
| **要するに** (*yōsuru ni*) | N2 | "To put it in a nutshell / In short" (distills a complex argument) | いろいろ言ったが、**要するに**賛成できないのだ。<br/>(*He gave many excuses, but in short, he cannot agree.*) |
| **なぜなら(ば)** (*nazenara[ba]*) | N3 | "Because / The reason is..." (must terminate in 〜からだ) | 旅行を取りやめた。**なぜなら**、台風が近づいている**からだ**。<br/>(*We canceled the trip. Because a typhoon is approaching.*) |

## 5. Topic Transitions (転換 / Tenkan)

Used to redirect the conversational or narrative flow:

- **さて** (*sate* — "Well now / Moving on to the main point"):
  - 天気の話はこのくらいにしましょう。**さて**、本日の本題に入ります。  
    (*So much for the weather. Now then, let's enter today's main topic.*)
- **ところで** (*tokorode* — "By the way" — completely changes the subject):
  - もうすぐお昼ですね。**ところで**、昨日のサッカー見ましたか？  
    (*It's almost noon. By the way, did you watch yesterday's soccer match?*)
- **では / それでは** (*dewa / soredewa* — "Well then / In that case"):
  - 皆さん揃いましたね。**それでは**、始めましょう。  
    (*Everyone has gathered. Well then, let us begin.*)

---

## JLPT Reading Comprehension Tip

> [!TIP]
> In JLPT N3 and N2 reading passages, **the author's actual opinion is almost always located immediately after contrastive connectors (しかし, だが) or summary connectors (つまり, 要するに)**.  
> Whenever you spot **しかし** or **つまり**, slow down and read the following sentence with maximum care—it frequently contains the answer to the reading comprehension question!
