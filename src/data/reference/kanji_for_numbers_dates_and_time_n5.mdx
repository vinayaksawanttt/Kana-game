# Kanji for Numbers, Dates & Time (数字・日付・時間)

Numbers, calendar dates, and time expressions are the most frequently tested kanji on the **JLPT N5** exam, especially because many dates and counters combine classical pictographs with **irregular readings**.

---

### Sound Changes in Hundreds (百) and Thousands (千)
- **300 (三百)**: **さんびゃく** (*san**bya**ku* — voiced *b*)
- **600 (六百)**: **ろっぴゃく** (*ro**ppya**ku* — small *っ* + *p*)
- **800 (八百)**: **はっぴゃく** (*ha**ppya**ku* — small *っ* + *p*)
- **3,000 (三千)**: **さんぜん** (*san**ze**n* — voiced *z*)
- **8,000 (八千)**: **はっせん** (*ha**ss**en* — small *っ*)

## 2. Days of the Week (曜日 / Yōbi)

Each day of the week is governed by an elemental Kanji:

| Kanji | Reading | Element | English | Daily Life Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **日曜日** | にちようび (*nichiyōbi*) | ☀️ Sun (日) | Sunday | Day off / Sunlight |
| **月曜日** | げつようび (*getsuyōbi*) | 🌙 Moon (月) | Monday | Start of work week |
| **火曜日** | かようび (*kayōbi*) | 🔥 Fire (火) | Tuesday | Mars day |
| **水曜日** | すいようび (*suiyōbi*) | 💧 Water (水) | Wednesday | Mid-week |
| **木曜日** | もくようび (*mokuyōbi*) | 🌳 Wood/Tree (木) | Thursday | Jupiter day |
| **金曜日** | きんようび (*kinyōbi*) | 🪙 Gold/Metal (金) | Friday | Payday / Money day |
| **土曜日** | どようび (*doyōbi*) | ⛰️ Earth/Soil (土) | Saturday | Weekend start |
| **何曜日** | なんようび (*nanyōbi*) | ❓ What day? | What day of week? | Question form |

## 5. Telling Time: Hours (〜時) & Minutes (〜分)

### Hours (〜時 / Ji) — Watch 4, 7, and 9!
- **4:00 (四時)**: **よじ** (*yoji* — drop the *n*!)
- **7:00 (七時)**: **しちじ** (*shichiji*)
- **9:00 (九時)**: **くじ** (*kuji*)

### Minutes (〜分 / Fun vs. Pun)
- **〜ぷん (*-pun*)** after **1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10**:
  - 1分: **いっぷん** (*ippun*)
  - 3分: **さんぷん** (*sanpun*)
  - 4分: **よんぷん** (*yonpun*)
  - 6分: **ろっぷん** (*roppun*)
  - 8分: **はっぷん** (*happun*)
  - 10分: **じゅっぷん / じっぷん** (*juppun / jippun*)
- **〜ふん (*-fun*)** after **2, 5, 7, 9**:
  - 2分: **にふん** (*nifun*), 5分: **ごふん** (*gofun*), 7分: **ななふん** (*nanafun*), 9分: **きゅうふん** (*kyūfun*)
- **Half-past (30 min)**: **半 (はん / *han*)**
  - 例: 九時半 (*kuji-han* = 9:30)
