# Japanese Body Parts Vocabulary (体の部位)

Mastering body parts (**体の部位 / *karada no bui*)** at the **JLPT N5** level is essential for describing physical appearance, visiting the doctor or pharmacy, and using foundational Japanese idioms.

---

> [!NOTE] 足 (*ashi*) vs. 脚 (*ashi*)
> While both are pronounced **あし (*ashi*)**:
> - **足** specifically refers to the **foot** (from the ankle down, including footwear).
> - **脚** specifically refers to the **entire leg** (from the thigh down to the ankle).  
> In beginner N5 writing, **足** is standard for both.

## 2. Essential Medical Symptom Collocations (病気・症状)

When visiting a Japanese clinic or pharmacy, body parts frequently combine with specific N5 verbs and adjectives:

### Pattern A: Expressing Pain with 〜が痛い (*~ ga itai*)
- **頭が痛いです。** (*Atama ga itai desu.* — I have a headache.)
- **お腹が痛いです。** (*Onaka ga itai desu.* — My stomach hurts.)
- **喉が痛いです。** (*Nodo ga itai desu.* — I have a sore throat.)
- **歯が痛いです。** (*Ha ga itai desu.* — I have a toothache.)

### Pattern B: Physical Ailment Verbs
| Japanese Phrase | Romaji | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **熱がある / 出る** | *netsu ga aru / deru* | To have a fever / fever develops |
| **風邪をひく** | *kaze o hiku* | To catch a cold |
| **咳が出る** | *seki ga deru* | To have a cough |
| **鼻水が出る** | *hanamizu ga deru* | To have a runny nose |
| **血が出る** | *chi ga deru* | To bleed |
| **骨を折る** | *hone o oru* | To break a bone (fracture) |

## 3. Describing Physical Appearance (外見の描写)

In Japanese, physical traits are described using the **[Body Part] が [Adjective]** pattern:

- **田中さんは背が高いです。**  
  *Tanaka-san wa se ga takai desu.*  
  (Mr. Tanaka is tall. *Lit: As for Tanaka, stature is high.*)
- **マリアさんは目が大きいです。**  
  *Maria-san wa me ga ōkii desu.*  
  (Maria has big eyes.)
- **妹は髪が長いです。**  
  *Imōto wa kami ga nagai desu.*  
  (My younger sister has long hair.)
- **山田さんは足が速いです。**  
  *Yamada-san wa ashi ga hayai desu.*  
  (Yamada is a fast runner. *Lit: His legs are fast.*)

## 4. Beginner Body Idioms (慣用句)

Japanese contains hundreds of colorful idiomatic expressions based on body parts:

| Idiom | Reading | Literal Meaning | Figurative Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **頭がいい** | *atama ga ii* | Head is good | Smart / Clever / Intelligent |
| **手伝う** | *tetsudau* | Accompany hands | To assist / To help out |
| **気に入る** | *ki ni iru* | Enters the spirit | To take a liking to / To enjoy |
| **口が軽い** | *kuchi ga karui* | Mouth is light | Talkative / Cannot keep a secret |
| **口が堅い** | *kuchi ga katai* | Mouth is hard | Tight-lipped / Keeps secrets well |
| **首になる** | *kubi ni naru* | To become a neck (beheaded) | To be fired / To be dismissed from work |
| **手を洗う** | *te o arau* | Wash hands | Wash hands; (fig.) To cut ties with bad deeds |
