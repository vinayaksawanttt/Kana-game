# Prefectures and Regions of Japan (日本の地方と都道府県)

Japan is an archipelago extending over 3,000 kilometers from northeast to southwest, comprising four primary main islands (**本州 — Honshu**, **北海道 — Hokkaido**, **九州 — Kyushu**, and **四国 — Shikoku**) alongside over 14,000 smaller islands.

Administratively, Japan is organized into **47 first-tier jurisdictions** known collectively as **都道府県 (*Todōfuken*)**, grouped into **8 traditional geographic regions (*Hachi-chihō Kubun*)**.

---

## 1. The Todōfuken System (1都 1道 2府 43県)

The 47 prefectures use four distinct administrative suffixes:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  • 都 (To):  東京都 (Tokyo Metropolis)            ─ 1  │
│  • 道 (Dō): 北海道 (Hokkaido Circuit)            ─ 1  │
│  • 府 (Fu): 大阪府 (Osaka) & 京都府 (Kyoto)       ─ 2  │
│  • 県 (Ken): All other 43 administrative units    ─ 43 │
│  Total: 47 Prefectures (都道府県 - Todōfuken)         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The 8 Geographic Regions (八地方区分)

```
[North]  北海道 (Hokkaido)
           │
         東北 (Tohoku)
           │
         関東 (Kanto) ──► Capital Metropolis (Tokyo/Yokohama)
           │
         中部 (Chubu) ──► Mt. Fuji & Japanese Alps
           │
         近畿 / 関西 (Kinki / Kansai) ──► Historic Heartland (Kyoto/Osaka/Nara)
           │
         中国 (Chugoku) ──► Seto Inland Sea (Hiroshima)
           │
         四国 (Shikoku) ──► 88 Temple Pilgrimage
           │
[South]  九州・沖縄 (Kyushu & Okinawa) ──► Volcanoes & Subtropical Isles
```

## 3. Comprehensive Breakdown of the 8 Regions

### 1. 北海道地方 (Hokkaido Region)
- **Prefecture**: 北海道 (*Hokkaidō*) | **Capital**: 札幌市 (*Sapporo*)
- **Geography & Climate**: Japan's second-largest island; vast agricultural plains, severe winters, national parks.
- **Key Cities & Icons**: 函館 (*Hakodate*), 小樽 (*Otaru*), 旭川 (*Asahikawa*), Sapporo Snow Festival (*Yuki Matsuri*).
- **Specialties**: Miso ramen, fresh crab/salmon/urchin, dairy products, lavender fields.

---

### 2. 東北地方 (Tōhoku Region — Northern Honshu)
Characterized by rugged mountain ranges, deep winter snowfalls (*Yukiguni*), hot spring onsen villages, and high-yield rice agriculture.

| Prefecture | Kanji / Capital | Famous For & Regional Specialties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aomori** | 青森県 (青森市) | Tsugaru apples, Nebuta Summer Lantern Festival, Hirosaki Castle sakura. |
| **Iwate** | 岩手県 (盛岡市) | Wanko soba, Sanriku seafood coast, Chūson-ji Golden Hall. |
| **Miyagi** | 宮城県 (仙台市) | Sendai (the "City of Trees"), Gyūtan (grilled beef tongue), Matsushima Bay. |
| **Akita** | 秋田県 (秋田市) | Akita dog breed, Akita Komachi rice, Kiritanpo hot pot, Kanto festival. |
| **Yamagata** | 山形県 (山形市) | Cherries (Satōnishiki), Yamadera mountain temple, Ginzan Onsen. |
| **Fukushima** | 福島県 (福島市) | Kitakata ramen, peaches, Aizu-Wakamatsu samurai history. |

---

### 3. 関東地方 (Kantō Region — Eastern Honshu)
The political, financial, cultural, and demographic engine of modern Japan, centered on the flat, sprawling Kanto Plain.

| Prefecture | Kanji / Capital | Key Cities & Cultural Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tokyo** | 東京都 (新宿区) | National capital; Shinjuku, Shibuya, Asakusa, Akihabara, Imperial Palace. |
| **Kanagawa** | 神奈川県 (横浜市) | Yokohama (Japan's 2nd largest city, Chinatown, Minato Mirai), Kamakura (Great Buddha), Hakone onsen. |
| **Saitama** | 埼玉県 (さいたま市) | Major residential corridor; historic Kawagoe ("Little Edo"), Omiya railway hub. |
| **Chiba** | 千葉県 (千葉市) | Narita International Airport, Tokyo Disney Resort, Boso Peninsula beaches. |
| **Ibaraki** | 茨城県 (水戸市) | Mito Kairakuen Garden, Tsukuba Science City, natto (fermented soybeans). |
| **Tochigi** | 栃木県 (宇都宮市) | Nikkō Tōshō-gū Shrine (UNESCO), Utsunomiya gyoza dumplings, Tochiotome strawberries. |
| **Gunma** | 群馬県 (前橋市) | Kusatsu Onsen (top hot spring), Mt. Haruna, Daruma doll craft. |

---

### 4. 中部地方 (Chūbu Region — Central Honshu)
Spanning the Japanese Alps and Mt. Fuji, Chubu is subdivided into **Hokuriku** (Sea of Japan coast), **Kōshin'etsu** (alpine central), and **Tōkai** (Pacific industrial coast).

- **愛知県 (Aichi / 名古屋市)**: Home to Toyota Motor Corp; Nagoya Castle, Hitsumabushi (eel), Miso katsu.
- **静岡県 (Shizuoka / 静岡市)**: Southern slopes of **富士山 (*Mt. Fuji*)**, green tea capital of Japan, wasabi cultivation.
- **山梨県 (Yamanashi / 甲府市)**: Northern slopes of Mt. Fuji, Fuji Five Lakes, Koshu wine, grapes and peaches.
- **長野県 (Nagano / 長野市)**: 1998 Winter Olympics, Zenkō-ji temple, Japanese Alps hiking, Shinshu soba.
- **新潟県 (Niigata / 新潟市)**: Premium Koshihikari rice, pure snowmelt sake breweries, Sado Island.
- **石川県 (Ishikawa / 金沢市)**: Historic castle town of Kanazawa, Kenroku-en Garden, Kutani porcelain, gold leaf.
- **富山県 (Toyama / 富山市)**: Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route (snow walls), Toyama Bay glass shrimp.
- **福井県 (Fukui / 福井市)**: Dinosaur fossil excavations, Eihei-ji Zen temple, Echizen crab.
- **岐阜県 (Gifu / 岐阜市)**: Shirakawa-gō thatched-roof farmhouses (UNESCO), Takayama old merchant streets.

---

### 5. 近畿・関西地方 (Kinki / Kansai Region — Western Honshu)
The historic and spiritual cradle of Japanese civilization, holding over a millennium of imperial history.

| Prefecture | Kanji / Capital | Historical & Culinary Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Osaka** | 大阪府 (大阪市) | "Nation's Kitchen" (*Tenka no Daidokoro*); Dotonbori, Osaka Castle, Takoyaki, Okonomiyaki. |
| **Kyoto** | 京都府 (京都市) | Imperial capital for 1,000+ years (794–1868); 2,000+ temples/shrines (Kinkaku-ji, Fushimi Inari). |
| **Hyōgo** | 兵庫県 (神戸市) | Cosmopolitan port of Kobe (Kobe beef), Himeji Castle (White Heron UNESCO fortress). |
| **Nara** | 奈良県 (奈良市) | First permanent imperial capital (710); Tōdai-ji Giant Bronze Buddha, wild sacred deer in Nara Park. |
| **Shiga** | 滋賀県 (大津市) | **琵琶湖 (*Lake Biwa*)** — Japan's largest freshwater lake; Omi wagyu beef, Hikone Castle. |
| **Wakayama** | 和歌山県 (和歌山市) | Sacred Mount Kōya (Kōyasan Shingon Buddhism), Kumano Kodō pilgrimage trails, Kishu umeboshi plums. |
| **Mie** | 三重県 (津市) | **伊勢神宮 (*Ise Grand Shrine*)** — the supreme Shinto shrine; Mikimoto pearl culture, Matsusaka beef. |

---

### 6. 中国地方 (Chūgoku Region — Southwestern Honshu)
Bordering the tranquil Seto Inland Sea (*Setonaikai*) to the south and the Sea of Japan to the north.
- **広島県 (Hiroshima / 広島市)**: Peace Memorial Park & Atomic Bomb Dome, Miyajima Island (Itsukushima "Floating" Torii Gate), Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki.
- **岡山県 (Okayama / 岡山市)**: Kōraku-en Garden, Kurashiki Bikan historical canal district, Momotarō folklore.
- **山口県 (Yamaguchi / 山口市)**: Shimonoseki fugu (pufferfish), Tsunoshima Bridge, Motonosumi Shrine red gates.
- **島根県 (Shimane / 松江市)**: **出雲大社 (*Izumo Taisha*)** — one of Japan's oldest Shinto shrines; Matsue Castle.
- **鳥取県 (Tottori / 鳥取市)**: Tottori Sand Dunes (*Tottori Sakyū*), 20th-century Asian pears.

---

### 7. 四国地方 (Shikoku Region — Smallest Main Island)
Famous for rural tranquility, rugged gorges, citrus orchards, and the ancient **88-Temple Henro Pilgrimage (四国八十八箇所)**.
- **香川県 (Kagawa / 高松市)**: "Udon Prefecture" — famous for firm **Sanuki udon**, Ritsurin Garden.
- **愛媛県 (Ehime / 松山市)**: **道後温泉 (*Dōgo Onsen*)** — Japan's oldest hot spring bathhouse; sweet mikan oranges.
- **徳島県 (Tokushima / 徳島市)**: World-famous **阿波踊り (*Awa Odori*)** dance festival, Naruto tidal whirlpools.
- **高知県 (Kōchi / 高知市)**: Samurai leader Sakamoto Ryōma, Katsuo no tataki (seared bonito), Shimanto River.

---

### 8. 九州・沖縄地方 (Kyushu & Okinawa Region — Southwest)
Characterized by active volcanism, lush subtropical warmth, and historic gateways for foreign trade.
- **福岡県 (Fukuoka / 福岡市)**: Economic hub of Kyushu; Hakata tonkotsu ramen, outdoor food stalls (*Yatai*), mentaiko.
- **熊本県 (Kumamoto / 熊本市)**: Kumamoto Castle, Mount Aso (one of the world's largest volcanic calderas), Kumamon mascot.
- **鹿児島県 (Kagoshima / 鹿児島市)**: Active volcano **桜島 (*Sakurajima*)**, Kurobuta black pork, Satsuma sweet potatoes.
- **長崎県 (Nagasaki / 長崎市)**: Historic international trading port (Dejima Dutch enclave), Peace Park, Champon noodles.
- **大分県 (Ōita / 大分市)**: The "Onsen Capital of Japan" — Beppu and Yufuin hot springs.
- **佐賀県 (Saga / 佐賀市)**: Arita and Imari ceramic pottery, Saga wagyu beef.
- **宮崎県 (Miyazaki / 宮崎市)**: Takachiho Gorge mythology, Miyazaki mangoes, Nichinan subtropical coast.
- **沖縄県 (Okinawa / 那覇市)**: Formerly the independent Ryūkyū Kingdom; Shuri Castle, coral reefs, Goya champurū.

## 4. Administrative Suffixes & Geography Vocabulary

| Japanese Term | Romaji | Meaning | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **都道府県** | *todōfuken* | Prefectures (nationwide) | 全国の都道府県 |
| **市** | *shi* | City | 横浜市 (*Yokohama-shi*) |
| **区** | *ku* | Ward / Borough (in major designated cities) | 新宿区 (*Shinjuku-ku*) |
| **町** | *chō / machi* | Town | 箱根町 (*Hakone-machi*) |
| **村** | *son / mura* | Village | 白川村 (*Shirakawa-mura*) |
| **県庁所在地** | *kenchō shozaichi* | Prefectural capital city | 青森県の県庁所在地は青森市 |
| **島** | *shima / jima* | Island | 本州、佐渡島、淡路島 |
| **半島** | *hantō* | Peninsula | 伊豆半島 (*Izu Hantō*) |
| **温泉** | *onsen* | Natural geothermal hot springs | 草津温泉、別府温泉 |
