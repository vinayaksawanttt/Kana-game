# JLPT N4 Grammar Patterns: Master Reference Sheet

The **JLPT N4** level represents the bridge from elementary survival Japanese to communicative autonomy. It introduces grammatical **voice** (passive, causative, potential), nuanced **conditionals**, **giving and receiving favors**, and expressions of **intention and conjecture**.

---

## 1. Verbal Voice: Potential, Passive & Causative

| Grammar Pattern | Formation Formula | Core Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Potential Form (可能形)** | G1: u-vowel → e-vowel + る (書ける)<br/>G2: drop る + られる (食べられる) | *Can do / Is able to do* | 日本語の新聞が**読めます**。<br/>(*I can read Japanese newspapers.*) |
| **Passive Voice (受身形)** | G1: u-vowel → a-vowel + れる (書かれる)<br/>G2: drop る + られる (食べられる) | *Is done by / Suffering passive* | 先生に名前を**呼ばれました**。<br/>(*I was called by the teacher.*)<br/>雨に**降られました**。 (*Rained upon / suffered.*) |
| **Causative Voice (使役形)** | G1: u-vowel → a-vowel + せる (書かせる)<br/>G2: drop る + させる (食べさせる) | *Make / Let someone do* | 母は子どもに野菜を**食べさせます**。<br/>(*The mother makes the child eat vegetables.*) |
| **Causative-Passive** | G1: a-vowel + される (行かされる)<br/>G2: drop る + させられる (食べさせられる) | *Be forced / made to do against will* | 嫌いな歌を**歌わせられました**。<br/>(*I was forced to sing a song I dislike.*) |

## 2. Favors: Giving & Receiving (授受表現)

```
[I/My group] ──あげる (give)──► [Other person]
[Other person] ──くれる (give to me)──► [Me/My in-group]
[I/My group] ◄──もらう (receive from)── [Other person]
```

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜てあげる** | Verb [て-form] + あげる | *Do a favor for someone* | 友達の荷物を持って**あげました**。<br/>(*I carried my friend's luggage for them.*) |
| **〜てくれる** | Verb [て-form] + くれる | *Someone does a favor for me / my group* | 田中さんが駅まで車で送って**くれました**。<br/>(*Tanaka kindly gave me a ride to the station.*) |
| **〜てもらう** | Verb [て-form] + もらう | *Receive the favor of someone doing for me* | 先生に日本語を直して**もらいました**。<br/>(*I had my teacher correct my Japanese.*) |
| **〜ていただけませんか** | Verb [て-form] + いただけませんか | *Could you please politely do for me?* | 書き方を教えて**いただけませんか**。<br/>(*Could you please kindly teach me how to write this?*) |

## 3. Conditionals: "If" and "When" (と, ば, たら, なら)

| Pattern | Formation | Core Nuance | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜と** | Verb [dictionary form] + と | Natural, unavoidable, or mechanical consequence ("whenever A, B follows") | このボタンを押す**と**、お釣りが出ます。<br/>(*Press this button, and the change comes out.*) |
| **〜ば** | G1: e-row + ば (行けば)<br/>G2: drop る + れば (食べれば) | Hypothetical condition ("provided that...") | 安けれ**ば**、買います。<br/>(*If it is cheap, I will buy it.*) |
| **〜たら** | Verb [た-form] + ら (行ったら) | Chronological sequence ("once/if A happens, then B") | 家に帰っ**たら**、お風呂に入ります。<br/>(*When I get home, I will take a bath.*) |
| **〜なら** | Noun / Plain + なら | Contextual assumption ("given that...", "as for...") | 京都へ行く**なら**、秋がおすすめです。<br/>(*If you are going to Kyoto, autumn is recommended.*) |
| **〜ても / 〜でも** | Verb [て-form] + も | Concession ("even if / even though...") | 雨が降っ**ても**、出かけます。<br/>(*Even if it rains, I will go out.*) |

## 4. Intentions, Plans & Decisions

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜つもりだ** | Verb [dict. or ない] + つもりだ | *Intend to / Plan to do* | 大学を卒業したら、留学する**つもりです**。<br/>(*After graduating, I intend to study abroad.*) |
| **〜予定だ** | Verb [dict.] + 予定だ / Noun + の予定だ | *Scheduled / planned to happen* | 明日、会議がある**予定です**。<br/>(*A meeting is scheduled for tomorrow.*) |
| **〜ようと思う (意向形)** | Verb [Volitional form] + と思う | *Am thinking of doing [spontaneous intent]* | 新しい車を**買おうと思っています**。<br/>(*I am thinking of buying a new car.*) |
| **〜ことにする** | Verb [dict. or ない] + ことにする | *Decide on doing [personal decision]* | 毎日運動する**ことにしました**。<br/>(*I have decided to exercise every day.*) |
| **〜ことになる** | Verb [dict. or ない] + ことになる | *It has been decided that [external decision]* | 来月から大阪へ転勤する**ことになりました**。<br/>(*It has been decided that I will transfer to Osaka.*) |

## 5. Conjecture, Appearance & Hearsay

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜そうだ (Appearance)** | Verb [ます-stem] + そうだ<br/>い-Adj [drop い] + そうだ | *Looks like / Appears about to...* | 雨が降り**そうだ**。<br/>(*It looks like it is about to rain.*)<br/>このリンゴは美味し**そうですね**。<br/>(*This apple looks delicious.*) |
| **〜そうだ (Hearsay)** | Plain Form + そうだ | *I heard that... / Rumor has it...* | 天気予報によると、明日は雪だ**そうだ**。<br/>(*According to the weather forecast, I heard it will snow.*) |
| **〜ようだ / 〜みたいだ** | Plain Form + ようだ / みたいだ | *Seems like / Resembles* | 外は寒い**ようだ** / **みたいだ**。<br/>(*It seems cold outside.*) |
| **〜らしい** | Plain Form + らしい (Noun direct) | *Seems like (based on hearsay/evidence)* | 彼は来月結婚する**らしい**。<br/>(*Apparently, he is getting married next month.*) |
| **〜かもしれない** | Plain Form + かもしれない | *Might / Maybe / Possibly* | 明日は雨が降る**かもしれません**。<br/>(*It might rain tomorrow.*) |
| **〜はずだ** | Plain Form + はずだ (な-adj: な; Noun: の) | *Should be / Ought to be (logical expectation)* | 田中さんは昨日届いたから、知っている**はずです**。<br/>(*Tanaka arrived yesterday, so he ought to know.*) |

## 6. Purpose: 〜ために vs. 〜ように

```
ために (In order to):  Volitional, controlled action (Same subject in both clauses)
ように (So that):       Non-volitional, state of ability, potential verb or negative
```

- **留学するために**、お金を貯めています。  
  *Ryūgaku suru tame ni, okane o tamete imasu.*  
  (In order to study abroad [volitional aim], I am saving money.)
- 後ろの人にも**聞こえるように**、大きな声で話してください。  
  *Ushiro no hito ni mo kikoeru yō ni, ōkina koe de hanashite kudasai.*  
  (Please speak in a loud voice **so that** people in the back can hear [state/potential].)

## 7. Ease, Difficulty & Excess

| Pattern | Formation | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜やすい** | Verb [ます-stem] + やすい | *Easy to do* | このペンはとても書き**やすい**です。<br/>(*This pen is very easy to write with.*) |
| **〜にくい** | Verb [ます-stem] + にくい | *Difficult / Hard to do* | この薬は苦くて、飲み**にくい**です。<br/>(*This medicine is bitter and hard to swallow.*) |
| **〜すぎる** | Verb [ます-stem] / Adj stem + すぎる | *Do too much / Excessively [Adj]* | 昨夜、お酒を飲み**すぎました**。<br/>(*I drank too much last night.*)<br/>この服は高**すぎる**。<br/>(*These clothes are too expensive.*) |
