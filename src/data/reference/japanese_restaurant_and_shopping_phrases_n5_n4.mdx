# Japanese Restaurant and Shopping Phrases (飲食店・買い物の実用フレーズ)

Dining out and shopping in Japan is renowned for exceptional customer care (**おもてなし — *Omotenashi***). Because retail and dining staff follow standardized scripts, learning key conversational exchanges allows you to navigate everything from small ramen bars and convenience stores to multi-course restaurants and department stores with ease.

---

## 1. Restaurant Entry and Seating (入店と席の案内)

Upon opening the door, staff greet customers with: **「いらっしゃいませ！何名様ですか？」** (*Irasshaimase! Nan-mei-sama desu ka?* — Welcome! How many people?)

```
Stating Your Party Size (人数を伝える):
• 1 person:  1人です (hitori desu)
• 2 people:  2人です (futari desu)
• 3 people:  3人です (san-nin desu)
• 4 people:  4人です (yo-nin desu - ⚠️ NOT "yon-nin")
• 5 people:  5人です (go-nin desu)
```

| Japanese Phrase | Romaji | Meaning & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **予約した[姓]です** | *Yoyaku shita [Name] desu* | I am [Name], I have a reservation. |
| **カウンター席でいいです** | *Kauntā seki de ii desu* | Counter seating is fine. |
| **テーブル席をお願いします** | *Tēburu seki o onegai shimasu* | A table seat, please. |
| **禁煙席をお願いします** | *Kin'en seki o onegai shimasu* | Non-smoking seats, please. |
| **ただいま満席です** | *Tadaima manseki desu* | We are currently completely full. |
| **こちらのお名前表にご記入ください** | *Kochira no o-namaehyō ni...* | Please write your name on the waiting list clipboard. |

## 2. Ordering Food & Drinks (注文のフレーズ)

To summon waitstaff, raise your hand slightly and project firmly: **「すみません！」 (*Sumimasen!*)**. In many modern izakayas and family restaurants, tables feature a call button (**呼び出しボタン — *yobidashi botan***).

| Goal | Japanese Phrase | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ask for menu** | **メニューをいただけますか。** | May I please have a menu? |
| **English menu** | **英語のメニューはありますか。** | Do you have an English menu? |
| **Ask for recommendation** | **おすすめは何ですか。** | What do you recommend? |
| **Point and order** | **これをお願いします。** (*Kore o onegai shimasu.*) | This one, please. |
| **Order multiple items** | **これと、これを1つずつください。** | One of this, and one of this, please. |
| **Same as that** | **あれと同じものをください。** | I'll have the same thing as that person. |
| **Free cold water** | **お冷（お水）をいただけますか。** | Could I please have a glass of cold water? |
| **Free hot tea** | **温かいお茶をお願いします。** | Could I please have some hot green tea? |

### Ordering with Native Japanese Counters (ひとつ、ふたつ…)
When ordering portions of dishes, use the universal native counter:
- **1つ (*hitotsu*)** — One
- **2つ (*futatsu*)** — Two
- **3つ (*mittsu*)** — Three
- **4つ (*yottsu*)** — Four
- **5つ (*itsutsu*)** — Five

## 3. Dietary Preferences and Allergies (アレルギー・苦手なもの)

To request that an ingredient be omitted, use the pattern: **[Ingredient] + 抜きでお願いします (*...nuki de onegai shimasu*)**.

```
[Ingredient] ＋ 抜きでお願いします (Without [Ingredient], please!)
• Wasabi:   わさび抜きでお願いします (Wasabi-nuki de onegai shimasu)
• Onions:   ネギ抜きでお願いします (Negi-nuki de onegai shimasu)
• Pork:     豚肉抜きでお願いします (Butaniku-nuki de onegai shimasu)
• Meat:     お肉抜きでお願いします (O-niku-nuki de onegai shimasu)
```

| Japanese Phrase | Romaji | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **私は〇〇アレルギーがあります。** | *Watashi wa [Item] arerugī ga arimasu.* | I am allergic to [Item]. |
| **これにエビやカニは入っていますか。** | *Kore ni ebi ya kani wa haitte imasu ka.* | Does this contain shrimp or crab? |
| **ベジタリアン（ヴィーガン）対応の料理はありますか。** | *Bejitarian taiō no ryōri wa arimasu ka.* | Do you have vegetarian/vegan compatible dishes? |

## 4. The Bill and Payment (お会計: O-kaikei)

In traditional Japanese dining, payments are processed at the front cash register near the entrance, not at the dining table (unless at high-end ryotei or bars). Take the small clipboard bill from your table to the cashier.

| Goal | Japanese Phrase | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ask for check** | **お会計をお願いします。** (*O-kaikei o onegai shimasu.*) | Check/bill, please. |
| **Separate checks** | **別々でお会計できますか。** (*Betsubetsu de o-kaikei dekimasu ka.*) | Can we pay separately? |
| **Pay together** | **一緒でお願いします。** (*Issho de onegai shimasu.*) | We'll pay together. |
| **Credit cards?** | **クレジットカードは使えますか。** (*Kurejitto kādo wa tsukaemasu ka.*) | Do you accept credit cards? |
| **Electronic cash?** | **電子マネー（交通系IC / PayPay）は使えますか。** | Do you accept IC cards / PayPay? |
| **Receipt please** | **レシート（領収書）をください。** (*Reshīto o kudasai.*) | Receipt, please. |
| **Thanking the chef** | **ごちそうさまでした！** (*Gochisōsama deshita!*) | Thank you for the wonderful meal! |

> [!NOTE]
> **No Tipping Culture (チップ不要)**:
> There is **zero tipping** in Japan. Leaving extra money on a table or counter will result in staff chasing you down the street to return your forgotten cash!

## 5. The Convenience Store Protocol (コンビニの4大定番質問)

Japanese convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) follow a predictable 4-question script at checkout:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             The 4 Standard Combini Questions           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. ポイントカードはお持ちですか？ (Points card?)         │
│    ➔ ないです (Nai desu — I don't have one)            │
│                                                        │
│ 2. お弁当、温めますか？ (Warm up your bento?)           │
│    ➔ お願いします (Yes please) / 大丈夫です (No thanks)│
│                                                        │
│ 3. お箸／スプーンはお付けしますか？ (Cutlery included?) │
│    ➔ お願いします (Yes please) / 結構です (No thanks)  │
│                                                        │
│ 4. レジ袋はご利用になりますか？ (Need a plastic bag?)   │
│    ➔ 1枚お願いします (1 bag please) / 大丈夫です (No)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 6. Retail & Shopping Phrases (買い物の表現)

| Situation | Japanese Phrase | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Store clerk approaches** | **見ているだけです。** (*Mite iru dake desu.*) | I'm just looking / browsing, thanks. |
| **Try on clothes** | **試着してもいいですか。** (*Shichaku shite mo ii desu ka.*) | May I try this on? |
| **Fitting room location** | **試着室はどこですか。** (*Shichakushitsu wa doko desu ka.*) | Where is the fitting room? |
| **Different size?** | **これの、もっと大きい（小さい）サイズはありますか。** | Do you have a larger (smaller) size of this? |
| **Different color?** | **別の色はありますか。** (*Betsu no iro wa arimasu ka.*) | Do you have this in another color? |
| **Gift wrapping** | **プレゼント用に包んでいただけますか。** | Could you gift wrap this for a present? |
| **Tax-free shopping** | **免税（タックスフリー）の手続きはできますか。** | Can I process tax-free shopping with my passport? |
