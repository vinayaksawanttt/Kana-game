# Japanese Days, Months, and Dates (曜日・月・日付)

Expressing dates in Japanese involves knowing three distinct components:
1. **Days of the Week (曜日 / *yōbi*)** — named after classical celestial bodies and elements.
2. **Months of the Year (月 / *gatsu*)** — structured logically with three essential phonetic exceptions.
3. **Days of the Month (日 / *hi, nichi, ka*)** — famously irregular for days 1 to 10, 14, 20, and 24 due to ancient native Japanese numerals (*Yamato-kotoba*).

---

## 1. Days of the Week (曜日 / Yōbi)

All days of the week end in **曜日 (ようび / *yōbi*)**. In casual schedules, calendars, and train timetables, they are frequently abbreviated to just the single first kanji in parentheses: `(月)`, `(火)`, `(水)`, etc.

| English | Full Form | Abbreviation | Hiragana | Romaji | Celestial / Elemental Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | **月曜日** | (月) | げつようび | *getsuyōbi* | 🌙 Moon (月) |
| **Tuesday** | **火曜日** | (火) | かようび | *kayōbi* | 🔥 Fire / Mars (火星) |
| **Wednesday** | **水曜日** | (水) | すいようび | *suiyōbi* | 💧 Water / Mercury (水星) |
| **Thursday** | **木曜日** | (木) | もくようび | *mokuyōbi* | 🌳 Wood / Tree / Jupiter (木星) |
| **Friday** | **金曜日** | (金) | きんようび | *kinyōbi* | 🪙 Gold / Metal / Venus (金星) |
| **Saturday** | **土曜日** | (土) | どようび | *doyōbi* | ⛰️ Earth / Soil / Saturn (土星) |
| **Sunday** | **日曜日** | (日) | にちようび | *nichiyōbi* | ☀️ Sun (太陽) |
| **What day?** | **何曜日** | — | なんようび | *nanyōbi* | Question: "What day of the week?" |

### Common Collocations:
- **平日** (へいじつ / *heijitsu*): Weekdays (Mon–Fri)
- **週末** (しゅうまつ / *shūmatsu*): Weekend (Sat–Sun)
- **定休日** (ていきゅうび / *teikyūbi*): Regular closing day (for shops/restaurants)

## 2. Months of the Year (〜月 / Gatsu)

Months in Japanese are formed by combining the digit (1–12) with the suffix **月 (がつ / *gatsu*)**.

> [!WARNING] The Three Crucial Irregular Months: 4, 7, and 9!
> The JLPT frequently tests the readings for April, July, and September. Do not use *yon-gatsu*, *nana-gatsu*, or *kyū-gatsu*!

| Month | Kanji | Hiragana | Romaji | Pronunciation Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **January** | 一月 | いちがつ | *ichigatsu* | Regular |
| **February** | 二月 | にがつ | *nigatsu* | Regular |
| **March** | 三月 | さんがつ | *sangatsu* | Regular |
| **April** | **四月** | **しがつ** | **shigatsu** | **Exception!** Uses *shi*, NOT *yongatsu* |
| **May** | 五月 | ごがつ | *gogatsu* | Regular |
| **June** | 六月 | ろくがつ | *rokugatsu* | Regular |
| **July** | **七月** | **しちがつ** | **shichigatsu** | **Exception!** Uses *shichi*, NOT *nanagatsu* |
| **August** | 八月 | はちがつ | *hachigatsu* | Regular |
| **September** | **九月** | **くがつ** | **kugatsu** | **Exception!** Uses *ku*, NOT *kyūgatsu* |
| **October** | 十月 | じゅうがつ | *jūgatsu* | Regular |
| **November** | 十一月 | じゅういちがつ | *jūichigatsu* | Regular |
| **December** | 十二月 | じゅうにがつ | *jūnigatsu* | Regular |
| **What month?** | **何月** | **なんがつ** | **nangatsu** | Question word |

> [!NOTE] Month of the Year vs. Duration of Months
> - Specific Month: **〜月 (がつ / *gatsu*)** (e.g., 三月 *sangatsu* = March)
> - Duration of Months: **〜ヶ月 / 〜か月 (かげつ / *kagetsu*)** (e.g., 三ヶ月 *san-kagetsu* = for 3 months)

## 3. Days of the Month (1st through 31st)

The readings for the days of the month mix ancient native readings (ending in 〜か / *-ka*) with regular Sino-Japanese number + にち (*nichi*).

| **26th** | 二十六日 | にじゅうろくにち | *nijūrokunichi* | Regular |
| **27th** | 二十七日 | にじゅうしちにち / にじゅうななにち | *nijūshichinichi / nijūnananichi* | *nijūshichinichi* preferred |
| **28th** | 二十八日 | にじゅうはちにち | *nijūhachinichi* | Regular |
| **29th** | 二十九日 | にじゅうくにち / にじゅうきゅうにち | *nijūkunichi / nijūkyūnichi* | *nijūkunichi* preferred |
| **30th** | 三十日 | さんじゅうにち | *sanjūnichi* | Regular |
| **31st** | 三十一日 | さんじゅういちにち | *sanjūichinichi* | Regular |
| **What date?**| **何日** | **なんにち** | **nannichi** | Question: "What day of the month?" |

> [!IMPORTANT] 一日: ついたち (*tsuitachi*) vs. いちにち (*ichinichi*)
> - **Calendar Date (1st of the month)**: Always read as **ついたち (*tsuitachi*)**.  
>   *例: 4月1日 (しがつついたち - April 1st)*
> - **Duration (1 day length)**: Always read as **いちにち (*ichinichi*)**.  
>   *例: 一日中 (いちにちじゅう - all day long)*; *一日待つ (いちにちまつ - to wait for one day)*

## 5. Japanese Date Word Order

Unlike English (Day/Month/Year or Month/Day/Year), Japanese always follows **broadest to most specific**:
```
[Year] 年 + [Month] 月 + [Day] 日 + ([Day of Week] 曜日)
```
- **Example**: 2024年10月15日(火)  
  *Nisen-nijū-yo-nen, jū-gatsu, jū-go-nichi (ka-yōbi)*  
  Tuesday, October 15, 2024.
