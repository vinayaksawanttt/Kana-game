# Japanese Numbers 0–10,000 and Big Numbers (数字)

Mastering Japanese numbers is essential for daily life—handling money (円 / *en*), shopping, dates, phone numbers, and JLPT listening and reading comprehension. 

While Western languages group large numbers in units of **thousands ($10^3 = 1,000$)**, Japanese groups large figures in units of **ten thousands ($10^4 = 10,000$ / 万 / *man*)**. Understanding this fundamental structural difference makes converting large sums intuitive.

## 2. Numbers 11 to 99: Additive & Multiplicative Stacking

Japanese tens follow strict base-10 mathematics:
- **11–19**: 十 + [digit] (e.g., 11 = 十一 *jū-ichi*, 14 = 十四 *jū-yon*, 17 = 十七 *jū-nana*).
- **20–90**: [digit] + 十 (e.g., 20 = 二十 *ni-jū*, 30 = 三十 *san-jū*, 90 = 九十 *kyū-jū*).
- **Compound tens**: [tens] + [single digit] (e.g., 35 = 三十五 *san-jū-go*, 99 = 九十九 *kyū-jū-kyū*).

## 3. Hundreds (100–900 / 百): Phonetic Sound Changes (Rendaku)

The base word for 100 is **百 (ひゃく / *hyaku*)**. Notice that **100 is just 百 (*hyaku*)**, NOT *ichi-hyaku*.

> [!WARNING] Sound Changes at 300, 600, and 800!
> - **300**: Voicing shift from *h* → **b** (**さんびゃく** / *sanbyaku*).
> - **600**: Geminate contraction + *p* (**ろっぴゃく** / *roppyaku*).
> - **800**: Geminate contraction + *p* (**はっぴゃく** / *happyaku*).

| Number | Kanji | Hiragana | Romaji | Phonetic Alternation |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **100** | 百 | ひゃく | *hyaku* | Base reading (no 一) |
| **200** | 二百 | にひゃく | *nihyaku* | Regular |
| **300** | **三百** | **さんびゃく** | **sanbyaku** | **Voiced (b)** |
| **400** | 四百 | よんひゃく | *yonhyaku* | Regular (*yon*) |
| **500** | 五百 | ごひゃく | *gohyaku* | Regular |
| **600** | **六百** | **ろっぴゃく** | **roppyaku** | **Small っ + p** |
| **700** | 七百 | ななひゃく | *nanahyaku* | Regular (*nana*) |
| **800** | **八百** | **はっぴゃく** | **happyaku** | **Small っ + p** |
| **900** | 九百 | きゅうひゃく | *kyūhyaku* | Regular (*kyū*) |

## 4. Thousands (1,000–9,000 / 千): Sound Changes

The base word for 1,000 is **千 (せん / *sen*)**. Just like hundreds, **1,000 is simply 千 (*sen*)**, NOT *ichi-sen*.

> [!WARNING] Sound Changes at 3,000 and 8,000!
> - **3,000**: Voiced shift from *s* → **z** (**さんぜん** / *sanzen*).
> - **8,000**: Geminate contraction (**はっせん** / *hassen*).

| Number | Kanji | Hiragana | Romaji | Phonetic Alternation |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1,000** | 千 | せん | *sen* | Base reading (no 一) |
| **2,000** | 二千 | にせん | *nisen* | Regular |
| **3,000** | **三千** | **さんぜん** | **sanzen** | **Voiced (z)** |
| **4,000** | 四千 | よんせん | *yonsen* | Regular (*yon*) |
| **5,000** | 五千 | ごせん | *gosen* | Regular |
| **6,000** | 六千 | ろくせん | *rokusen* | Regular |
| **7,000** | 七千 | ななせん | *nanasen* | Regular (*nana*) |
| **8,000** | **八千** | **はっせん** | **hassen** | **Small っ (double s)** |
| **9,000** | 九千 | きゅうせん | *kyūsen* | Regular (*kyū*) |

## 5. Ten Thousands and the 4-Digit Myriad System (万, 億, 兆)

In Western notation, commas divide numbers every **3 zeroes (thousands)**:
$$1,000 \quad 1,000,000 \quad 1,000,000,000$$

In Japanese, counting groups shift every **4 zeroes (myriads)**:
$$10^4 = \text{万} (man) \qquad 10^8 = \text{億} (oku) \qquad 10^{12} = \text{兆} (chō)$$

> [!IMPORTANT] You MUST say "一万" (ichiman), never just "万"!
> Unlike 100 (*hyaku*) and 1,000 (*sen*), for 10,000 you **must** include 一 (*ichi*): **一万 (いちまん / *ichiman*)**.

| Value | Arabic Notation | Japanese Breakdown | Kanji | Reading |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10,000** | 10,000 | 1 万 | 一万 | いちまん (*ichiman*) |
| **20,000** | 20,000 | 2 万 | 二万 | にまん (*niman*) |
| **100,000** | 100,000 | 10 万 | 十万 | じゅうまん (*jūman*) |
| **1,000,000** | 1,000,000 | 100 万 | 百万 | ひゃくまん (*hyakuman*) |
| **10,000,000** | 10,000,000 | 1,000 万 | 一千万 | いっせんまん (*issenman*) |
| **100,000,000** | 100,000,000 | 1 億 ($10^8$) | 一億 | いちおく (*ichioku*) |
| **1,000,000,000** | 1,000,000,000 | 10 億 | 十億 | じゅうおく (*jūoku*) |
| **1,000,000,000,000** | 1,000,000,000,000 | 1 兆 ($10^{12}$) | 一兆 | いっちょう (*icchō*) |

### Converting Large Numbers Tip:
To convert any number from Western commas to Japanese 万:
1. Move 4 digits to the left.
2. Example: **¥350,000** → 35 | 0000 = **35万 (さんじゅうごまん - sanjūgoman)**.
3. Example: **¥2,800,000** → 280 | 0000 = **280万 (にひゃくはちじゅうまん - nihyaku hachijūman)**.

## 6. Prices, Shopping, and Money Expressions

- **Currency Unit**: **円 (えん / *en*)** (Yen).
- **Asking the price**:
  - これはいくらですか。 (*Kore wa ikura desu ka?* — How much is this?)
  - 全部でいくらですか。 (*Zenbu de ikura desu ka?* — How much is it in total?)
- **Useful Shopping Vocabulary**:
  - **消費税** (しょうひぜい / *shōhizei*): Consumption tax (sales tax)
  - **税込み** (ぜいこみ / *zeikomi*): Tax included
  - **税抜き** (ぜいぬき / *zeinuki*): Tax excluded
  - **おつり** (*otsuri*): Change (money returned)
  - **レシート** (*reshīto*): Receipt
