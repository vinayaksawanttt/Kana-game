# JLPT N2 Grammar Patterns: Master Reference Sheet

The **JLPT N2** level tests advanced functional Japanese used in professional business settings, news commentary, and formal essays. It requires mastery of **formal discourse markers**, **nuanced concessions**, **discrepancies between expectation and reality**, and **inescapable compulsions**.

---

## 1. Timing, Occasion & Progression

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Core Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜に際して / 〜にあたって** | Verb [dict.] / Noun + に際して | *On the occasion of / Prior to embarking on [formal occasion]* | 契約の締結**にあたって**、細部を確認した。<br/>(*On the occasion of concluding the contract, we reviewed the details.*) |
| **〜次第 (しだい)** | Verb [ます-stem] + 次第 | *As soon as [action completes, will do subsequent action]* | 詳しい日程が決まり**次第**、ご連絡いたします。<br/>(*As soon as the detailed schedule is decided, we will contact you.*) |
| **〜つつある** | Verb [ます-stem] + つつある | *In the gradual, continuous process of changing* | 景気は少しずつ回復し**つつある**。<br/>(*The economy is in the process of gradually recovering.*) |
| **〜一方だ (いっぽうだ)** | Verb [dict.] + 一方だ | *Steadily continuing to change in one direction (often negative)* | 円安の影響で、輸入品の価格は上がる**一方だ**。<br/>(*Due to the weak yen, prices of imported goods are steadily rising.*) |
| **〜うちに** | Verb [dict. / ない] / Noun + の + うちに | *While [condition persists] / Before [situation changes]* | 明るい**うちに**、山を下りましょう。<br/>(*While it is still light out, let's head down the mountain.*) |

## 2. Discrepancy & Concession (Contrast with Expectations)

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Nuance & Comparison | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜わりに(は)** | Plain Form + わりに(は)<br/>(Noun: の / な-adj: な) | *Considering that... surprisingly [unexpected result relative to price/age/etc.]* | この店は値段の**わりには**美味しい。<br/>(*Considering the low price, this restaurant is surprisingly delicious.*) |
| **〜にしては** | Plain Form / Noun + にしては | *For a [subgroup / category], contrary to the expected standard* | 彼は日本に10年住んでいる**にしては**、日本語が下手だ。<br/>(*For someone who has lived in Japan 10 years, his Japanese is poor.*) |
| **〜ものの** | Plain Form + ものの<br/>(な-adj: な) | *Although it is true that [A], nonetheless [B]* | 免許は取った**ものの**、一度も運転していない。<br/>(*Although I got my license, I have not driven even once.*) |
| **〜にもかかわらず** | Plain / Noun + にもかかわらず | *Despite / In spite of the fact that... (formal)* | 雨が激しい**にもかかわらず**、試合は続行された。<br/>(*Despite the torrential rain, the match was continued.*) |
| **〜反面 (はんめん)** | Plain Form + 反面<br/>(な-adj: な / である) | *While [A] is true, on the other hand conversely [B]* | 一人暮らしは自由な**反面**、寂しさを感じることもある。<br/>(*Living alone is liberating; on the other hand, one sometimes feels lonely.*) |

## 3. Inevitability, Compulsion & Lack of Alternatives

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜ざるを得ない** | Verb [ない-stem] + ざるを得ない<br/>(する → せざるを得ない) | *Have no choice but to do / Compelled by circumstance* | これだけの証拠があれば、事実を認め**ざるを得ない**。<br/>(*With this much evidence, one cannot help but admit the facts.*) |
| **〜ほかない / 〜よりほかない** | Verb [dict.] + ほかない | *Have no alternative but to...* | バスがないので、歩いて帰る**ほかない**。<br/>(*Since there are no buses, there is no choice but to walk home.*) |
| **〜にほかならない** | Noun / Plain + からにほかならない | *Is nothing other than / Is precisely because of...* | 今回の成功は、チーム全員の努力の賜物**にほかならない**。<br/>(*This success is nothing other than the fruit of the entire team's hard work.*) |
| **〜ずにはいられない** | Verb [ない-stem] + ずにはいられない<br/>(する → せずにはいられない) | *Cannot suppress the urge to / Can't help doing* | その悲しい知らせを聞いて、泣か**ずにはいられなかった**。<br/>(*Hearing that tragic news, I couldn't help but weep.*) |

## 4. Potential Risk & Diplomatic Refusal

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Nuance & Function | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜かねる** | Verb [ます-stem] + かねる | *Cannot bring oneself to do / Politely unable to (business refusal)* | そのご提案には賛成いたし**かねます**。<br/>(*I am politely unable to agree to that proposal.*) |
| **〜かねない** | Verb [ます-stem] + かねない | *Might very well happen / Poses a serious danger of...* | スピードの出しすぎは、大事故につながり**かねない**。<br/>(*Excessive speed could very well lead to a catastrophic accident.*) |
| **〜ようがない** | Verb [ます-stem] + ようがない | *There is no possible method or way to do [A]* | 連絡先が分からないので、連絡の**しようがない**。<br/>(*Not knowing his contact info, there is simply no way to contact him.*) |
| **〜げ** | Adj stem / Verb [ます-stem] + げ | *Bearing an outward air or appearance of [emotion]* | 彼女は寂し**げ**に微笑んだ。<br/>(*She smiled with an air of loneliness.*) |

## 5. Scope & Universality

| Pattern | Connection Formula | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **〜を問わず (をとわず)** | Noun + を問わず | *Regardless of / Irrespective of [criteria]* | 経験の有無**を問わず**、どなたでも応募できます。<br/>(*Regardless of experience, anyone may apply.*) |
| **〜にかかわらず** | Noun / Verb pairs + にかかわらず | *Regardless of / Whether or not* | 参加するしない**にかかわらず**、ご連絡ください。<br/>(*Whether participating or not, please contact us.*) |
| **〜ばかりに** | Plain Form + ばかりに | *Simply on account of [one small cause, a dire result happened]* | 嘘をついた**ばかりに**、信用を失ってしまった。<br/>(*Simply because I told a lie, I ended up losing all trust.*) |
| **〜だけに / だけのことはある** | Plain Form + だけに | *Precisely because / Fittingly, as expected of...* | 一流ホテル**だけに**、サービスが素晴らしい。<br/>(*Precisely because it's a top-tier hotel, the service is magnificent.*) |
