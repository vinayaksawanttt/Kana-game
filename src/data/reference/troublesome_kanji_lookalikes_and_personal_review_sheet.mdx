# Troublesome Kanji Lookalikes and Personal Review Sheet (紛らわしい漢字と弱点克服)

In Japanese kanji study, certain characters cause persistent review failures (often called **"leeches"** in Spaced Repetition Systems). These characters fail because their visual components are almost identical, or because they share identical On'yomi readings with contrasting meanings.

This reference card isolates the most notoriously confused kanji pairs across N5–N1, demonstrates how to dissect them by radical, and gives you a systematic diagnostic review sheet.

---

## 1. Visual Twins & Stroke-Length Traps (形が似ている漢字)

### A. The Single-Stroke Differences
- **未 (*mi*)** vs. **末 (*matsu / sue*)**:
  - **未** (Not yet / Incomplete): The **top horizontal line is SHORTER** than the bottom line. Represents a tree whose branches haven't fully grown (*mirai* — 未来 — future).
  - **末** (End / Tip): The **top horizontal line is LONGER** than the bottom line. Represents the very tip of a fully extended tree branch (*shūmatsu* — 週末 — weekend).
- **人 (*hito*)** vs. **入 (*iru / hairu*)**:
  - **人** (Person): Left stroke starts higher and supports the right stroke.
  - **入** (Enter): Right stroke starts higher and sweeps over the left stroke.

### B. The "Same Right-Side Component" Family

When kanji share the exact same phonetic right-hand side, look exclusively at the **semantic radical on the left**:

#### 1. The 寺 (*Temple*) Trio: 待 vs. 持 vs. 特

| Kanji | Radical (Left) | Radical Meaning | Whole Kanji Meaning | Key Vocabulary |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **待** | **彳** (*gyōninben*) | Stepping / Road / Movement | **To Wait** (standing on a path) | 待つ (*matsu*), 期待 (*kitai* — expectation) |
| **持** | **扌** (*tehen*) | Hand / Manual action | **To Hold / Possess** | 持つ (*motsu*), 気持ち (*kimochi* — feelings) |
| **特** | **牜** (*ushihen*) | Cow / Livestock | **Special / Unique** | 特別 (*tokubetsu* — special), 特急 (*tokkyū*) |

---

#### 2. The 木 / 冖 Family: 探 vs. 深

| Kanji | Radical (Left) | Meaning | Contrast Mnemonic |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **探** | **扌** (Hand) | **Search / Seek** (*sagasu / tan*) | You use your **HAND** (扌) to search under the trees ➔ 探す (*sagasu*), 探検 (*tanken* — exploration). |
| **深** | **氵** (Water) | **Deep / Profound** (*fukai / shin*) | Deep **WATER** (氵) under the trees ➔ 深い (*fukai*), 深夜 (*shin'ya* — late night). |

---

#### 3. The 東 (*East*) Family: 練 vs. 棟 vs. 錬

| Kanji | Left Radical | Radical Domain | Meaning | Example |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **練** | **糸** (*itohen*) | Silk / Thread | **Practice / Knead / Train** | 練習 (*renshū* — practice), 訓練 (*kunren*) |
| **棟** | **木** (*kihen*) | Wood / Timber | **Ridgepole / Building counter** | 1棟 (*ittō* — one building), 病棟 (*byōtō* — hospital ward) |
| **錬** | **金** (*kanehen*) | Metal / Gold | **Tempering / Forging** | 錬金術 (*renkinjutsu* — alchemy), 百戦錬磨 (*hyakusenremma*) |

---

#### 4. The 疑 (*Doubt*) Family: 疑 vs. 擬 vs. 凝

| Kanji | Added Component | Domain | Meaning | Example |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **疑** | Base character | Doubt / Suspicion | **Doubt / Question** | 疑問 (*gimon* — doubt/question), 容疑者 (*yōgisha* — suspect) |
| **擬** | **扌** (Hand) | Manual imitation | **Pseudo / Imitate** | 擬音語 (*giongo* — onomatopoeia), 模擬試験 (*mogi shiken* — mock exam) |
| **凝** | **冫** (Ice) | Freezing / Stiffening | **Stiff / Freeze / Concentrate** | 肩が凝る (*kata ga koru* — stiff shoulders), 凝縮 (*gyōshuku*) |

### C. Advanced Lookalikes: 微 vs. 徴 vs. 徹 (N2–N1)

These three advanced characters share the walking radical **彳** on the left and the hand action radical **攵** on the right, but their middle component differs:

```
• 微 (Micro / Subtle):  彳 ＋ 山 ＋ 几 ＋ 一 ＋ 攵 ➔ 微妙 (bimyō - subtle), 微生物 (microbe)
• 徴 (Sign / Levy):     彳 ＋ 山 ＋ 王 ＋ 攵     ➔ 特徴 (tokuchō - feature), 象徴 (symbol)
• 徹 (Thorough / Penetrate): 彳 ＋ 育 ＋ 攵       ➔ 徹底 (tettei - thorough), 徹夜 (all-nighter)
```

## 2. The Commerce & Money Family: 買 vs. 貝 vs. 員 vs. 貸 vs. 借

All characters involving finance and transactions originate from the cowrie shell radical **貝**:

```
           ┌──► 貝 (kai)     = Shell / Ancient currency
           ├──► 買 (kau)     = Net (罒) over shell ➔ BUY / Purchase
貝 (Shell) ├──► 員 (in)      = Mouth (口) over shell ➔ Employee / Member
           ├──► 貸 (kasu)    = Substitute (代) for money ➔ LEND to others
           └──► 借 (kariru)  = Person (亻) + Past (昔) ➔ BORROW from others
```

| Kanji | Reading | English | Usage Sentence |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **買** | *kau / bai* | Buy | スーパーで野菜を**買う**。 (*KAU*) |
| **貸** | *kasu / tai* | Lend | 友だちにノートを**貸す**。 (*KASU* — I give it out) |
| **借** | *kariru / shaku* | Borrow | 銀行からお金を**借りる**。 (*KARIRU* — I take it in) |
| **賃** | *chin* | Rent / Fare | 家**賃** (*yachin* — apartment rent), 運**賃** (*unchin* — transport fare) |

## 3. Homophone On'yomi Twins (同音異義の漢字)

When two different kanji words share the identical On'yomi pronunciation:

| Word Pair | Readings | Contrast & Distinction | Context Sentences |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **創造** vs. **想像** | *sōzō* | **創造** = Physical Creation (from scratch)<br/>**想像** = Mental Imagination (in mind) | 新しい価値を**創造**する (Create value)<br/>未来の生活を**想像**する (Imagine future) |
| **機会** vs. **機械** | *kikai* | **機会** = Intangible Opportunity / Chance<br/>**機械** = Physical Mechanical Machine | チャンスの**機会**を逃す (Miss opportunity)<br/>工場の**機械**が故障した (Factory machine broke) |
| **関心** vs. **感心** | *kanshin* | **関心** = Intellectual Interest / Concern<br/>**感心** = Emotional Admiration / Impressed | 政治に**関心**がある (Interested in politics)<br/>彼の努力に**感心**した (Admirably impressed) |

## 4. The "Leech Protocol": How to Master Stubborn Kanji

If an individual kanji fails repeatedly in your app or SRS flashcard reviews:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             The 3-Step Leech Elimination Protocol      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 1: STOP reviewing the single isolated character.  │
│         Isolated drilling breeds recognition illusions. │
│                                                        │
│ Step 2: Bind the kanji into a 2-word CONTRAST PHRASE.  │
│         e.g. 不要な段ボールを [押す] (Push box)         │
│              背筋をピンと    [伸ばす] (Stretch spine)   │
│                                                        │
│ Step 3: Color-code the distinguishing semantic radical:│
│         扌 (Hand action) vs. 亻 (Human state).          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
