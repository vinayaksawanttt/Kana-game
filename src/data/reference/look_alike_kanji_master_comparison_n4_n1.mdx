# Look-Alike Kanji Master Comparison (似た漢字の使い分け)

Every JLPT level deliberately features multiple-choice questions designed to catch test-takers who recognize kanji only by vague visual impressions. Distinguishing look-alike kanji requires focusing on three micro-features:
1. **Stroke length differences** (which horizontal line is longer).
2. **Piercing vs. contained strokes** (whether a stroke punches through an outer line).
3. **Radical / semantic classifier substitutions**.

---

## 1. Stroke Length, Angles & Piercing Strokes (JLPT N5–N3)

| Pair | Kanji A (Reading & Meaning) | Kanji B (Reading & Meaning) | Visual Discriminator & Mnemonic |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **末 vs. 未** | **末** (*matsu / sue*)<br>**End / Tip**<br>週末 (*shūmatsu* - weekend) | **未** (*mi / mada*)<br>**Not yet / Future**<br>未来 (*mirai* - future) | Look at the **top horizontal branch**:<br>• **末 (End)**: Top branch is **longer**—the tree is fully grown, reaching its *end*.<br>• **未 (Not yet)**: Top branch is **shorter**—the young branch is *not yet* fully grown. |
| **土 vs. 士** | **土** (*do / tsuchi*)<br>**Earth / Soil**<br>土地 (*tochi* - land) | **士** (*shi*)<br>**Warrior / Scholar**<br>武士 (*bushi*), 弁護士 (*bengoshi*) | Look at which horizontal bar is wider:<br>• **土 (Earth)**: **Bottom bar is wider** (solid ground foundation).<br>• **士 (Warrior)**: **Top bar is wider** (broad, muscular shoulders of a samurai). |
| **千 vs. 干** | **千** (*sen / chi*)<br>**Thousand**<br>千円 (*sen-en*) | **干** (*kan / ho-su*)<br>**Dry / Air out**<br>干す (*hosu* - to dry) | Look at the very top stroke:<br>• **千 (1,000)**: Starts with a slanted downward sweep (**ノ**).<br>• **干 (Dry)**: Starts with a flat, level horizontal bar (**一**) like a clothesline pole. |
| **牛 vs. 午** | **牛** (*gyū / ushi*)<br>**Cow / Cattle**<br>牛乳 (*gyūnyū* - milk) | **午** (*go*)<br>**Noon**<br>午前 (*gozen* - AM) | Look at the center vertical stroke:<br>• **牛 (Cow)**: Pierces all the way through the top bar like a **cow's horn**.<br>• **午 (Noon)**: Does **not** pierce the top bar; stops flush. |
| **刀 vs. 力** | **刀** (*tō / katana*)<br>**Sword / Blade**<br>日本刀 (*nihontō*) | **力** (*ryoku / chikara*)<br>**Power / Strength**<br>努力 (*doryoku* - effort) | Look at the upper-right corner:<br>• **刀 (Sword)**: Vertical sweep stays cleanly **inside** the top bar.<br>• **力 (Power)**: Vertical sweep **punches through the top** like a flexed bicep. |
| **右 vs. 石** | **右** (*u / migi*)<br>**Right**<br>右側 (*migigawa*) | **石** (*seki / ishi*)<br>**Stone / Rock**<br>石油 (*sekiyu* - oil) | Look at the diagonal stroke:<br>• **右 (Right)**: Diagonal stroke begins **above and pierces through** the horizontal bar.<br>• **石 (Stone)**: Diagonal stroke begins **tucked directly beneath** the flat cliff top. |
| **大 vs. 犬 vs. 太** | **大** (*dai / ō-kii* - big) | **犬** (*ken / inu* - dog)<br>**太** (*tai / futo-i* - fat) | • **犬 (Dog)**: Has a small dot (a stray flea!) on the **upper-right** shoulder.<br>• **太 (Fat)**: Has a dot placed low between its legs (extra girth/weight). |
| **日 vs. 白 vs. 目 vs. 自** | **日** (*hi* - sun/day)<br>**白** (*shiro* - white) | **目** (*me* - eye)<br>**自** (*ji* - oneself) | • **日**: Sun (1 middle line).<br>• **白**: White (Sun + top ray of light).<br>• **目**: Eye (2 horizontal pupil lines).<br>• **自**: Oneself (Eye + top dot pointing to one's own nose). |

## 2. The Famous Triplets: 己 vs. 已 vs. 巳 (JLPT N4–N1)

These three kanji differ only in the vertical height of the bottom-left stroke:

```
  ┌──────┐       ┌──────┐       ┌──────┐
  │ 己   │       │ 已   │       │ 巳   │
  │      │       │  │   │       │  │   │
  └──────┘       └──────┘       └──────┘
  Fully OPEN     HALF-CLOSED    FULLY CLOSED
```

| Kanji | Readings | Meaning | Visual Key | Vocabulary Example |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **己** | コ、キ / おのれ (*onore*) | **Self / Oneself** | **Fully open**: Your *self* is receptive and open. | 自己紹介 (*jikoshōkai* - self-introduction) |
| **已** | イ / すで・に (*sudeni*) | **Already / Stop** | **Half-closed**: Rises halfway up the opening. | 已に (*sudeni* - already [literary N1]) |
| **巳** | シ / み (*mi*) | **Snake (Zodiac)** | **Completely sealed**: Coiled up tight like a snake's head. | 巳年 (*midoshi* - Year of the Snake) |

## 3. High-Frequency Radical Substitutions (JLPT N4–N2)

| Pair | Kanji A (Radical & Clue) | Kanji B (Radical & Clue) | Memory Hook |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **待 vs. 持** | **待** (*tai / ma-tsu* - wait)<br>Radical: **彳 (street / pace)** | **持** (*ji / mo-tsu* - hold)<br>Radical: **扌 (hand)** | You **wait (待)** on the street (彳); you **hold (持)** with your hand (扌). |
| **開 vs. 閉** | **開** (*kai / a-keru* - open)<br>Inside: **廾 (two open hands)** | **閉** (*hei / shi-meru* - close)<br>Inside: **才 (wooden bolt)** | Two hands (**廾**) pull the gates open (開); a bar (**才**) bolts the gates shut (閉). |
| **貸 vs. 借** | **貸** (*tai / ka-su* - lend)<br>Top: **代** (substitute) + **貝** (money) | **借** (*shaku / ka-riru* - borrow)<br>Left: **亻** (person) + **昔** (formerly) | To **lend (貸)** is to provide an economic substitute; to **borrow (借)** requires asking a person. |
| **働 vs. 動** | **働** (*dō / hatara-ku* - work)<br>Radical: **亻 (person)** | **動** (*dō / ugo-ku* - move)<br>No person radical | A **person (亻)** exerting physical motion is **working (働)**. |
| **族 vs. 旅** | **族** (*zoku* - family/clan)<br>Bottom-right: **矢 (arrow)** | **旅** (*ryo / tabi* - travel)<br>Bottom-right: **衣-lower (garments)** | Warriors holding arrows (**矢**) under a banner protect the **family (族)**; travelers lift their robes to **journey (旅)**. |
| **鳥 vs. 烏** | **鳥** (*chō / tori* - bird)<br>Eye is drawn (**with center stroke**) | **烏** (*u / karasu* - crow/raven)<br>**Missing center eye stroke!** | A crow's plumage is so pitch-black you cannot see its eye inside its head! |
| **氷 vs. 永** | **氷** (*hyō / kōri* - ice)<br>Has a freezing drop on top-left | **永** (*ei / naga-i* - eternity)<br>Has continuous flowing strokes | Water (**水**) freezes into **ice (氷)**; an endless spring flows into **eternity (永)**. |

## 4. Advanced N2–N1 Complex Look-Alikes

| Confusing Family | Character Breakdown & Subtle Distinctions |
| :---: | :--- |
| **微 vs. 徴 vs. 懲** | • **微** (*bi* - delicate / subtle / micro, 微妙): Center has **几/兀**.<br>• **徴** (*chō* - symptom / indication / collect, 特徴): Center has **王 (king)**.<br>• **懲** (*chō* - chastise / penalize, 懲罰): Identical to 徴, but adds **心 (heart)** at the bottom! |
| **哀 vs. 衰 vs. 衷** | All three split the clothing radical (**衣**) into top and bottom halves:<br>• **哀** (*ai / awa-re* - sorrow / grief, 哀悼): Encloses a crying **口 (mouth)**.<br>• **衰** (*sui / otoro-eru* - decline / wither, 衰退): A horizontal bar pierces through the center.<br>• **衷** (*chū* - inner heart / moderation, 折衷): Encloses **中 (center/inside)**. |
| **壁 vs. 癖 vs. 避** | All three share the complex phonetic component **辟** (*heki*):<br>• **壁** (*heki / kabe* - masonry wall): Has **土 (earth/stone)** at the base.<br>• **癖** (*heki / kuse* - idiosyncratic habit / quirk): Wrapped in **疒 (sickness/compulsion)**.<br>• **避** (*hi / sa-keru* - dodge / evade, 避難): Wrapped in **辶 (road/escape)**. |
| **網 vs. 綱 vs. 鋼** | • **網** (*mō / ami* - fishing/communication net): Left radical is **糸 (thread)**; right is **罔**.<br>• **綱** (*kō / tsuna* - heavy hawser rope /綱領): Left radical is **糸 (thread)**; right is **岡 (sturdy ridge)**.<br>• **鋼** (*kō / hagane* - tempered steel): Left radical is **金 (metal)**. |
