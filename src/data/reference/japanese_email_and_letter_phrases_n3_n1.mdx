# Japanese Business Email and Letter Writing Phrases (ビジネスメール・手紙)

Written business correspondence in Japan follows rigorous conventions. While traditional letters adhere to centuries-old formulas of seasonal greetings and paired head/closing words, modern business emails have adapted these customs into crisp, predictable, and polite digital communication.

---

## 1. Anatomy of a Standard Japanese Business Email

A Japanese business email comprises **six mandatory components**, arranged in strict order:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. 件名 (Subject Line)                                 │
│    【ご確認依頼】新プロジェクト仕様書送付の件 (株式会社ABC・山田)│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. 宛名 (Recipient Address Block)                       │
│    株式会社グローバルテック                             │
│    営業部 部長                                         │
│    鈴木 一郎 様                                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. 挨拶・名乗り (Opening Greeting & Self-Identification) │
│    いつも大変お世話になっております。                   │
│    株式会社ABC 開発部の山田太郎でございます。           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. 本文 (Body & Specific Requests)                     │
│    標記の件につきまして、仕様書が完成いたしましたので   │
│    添付ファイルにてお送りいたします。                   │
│                                                        │
│    恐れ入りますが、内容をご確認のうえ、                 │
│    今週金曜日（15日）までにご返信いただけますでしょうか。│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. 結びの言葉 (Closing Sign-Off)                       │
│    お忙しいところ誠に恐縮ですが、                       │
│    何卒よろしくお願い申し上げます。                     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 6. 署名 (Signature Block)                              │
│    --------------------------------------------------  │
│    株式会社ABC 開発部                                  │
│    山田 太郎 (Taro Yamada)                             │
│    Email: yamada@abc-corp.example.com                  │
│    Tel: 03-1234-5678                                   │
│    --------------------------------------------------  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Essential Email Phrases by Section

### A. Subject Lines (件名: Kenmei)
Use square brackets `【 】` to categorize the email purpose at a glance:
- **【ご相談】** 新規プロジェクトのスケジュールについて — [Consultation] About new project schedule
- **【ご確認依頼】** 4月度お見積書送付の件 — [Confirmation Request] April estimate delivery
- **【日程調整】** お打ち合わせ候補日時のご案内 — [Scheduling] Meeting candidate dates
- **【重要】** サーバーメンテナンスに伴う一時停止のお知らせ — [Important] Maintenance downtime notice
- **【御礼】** 本日のご来社のお礼 — [Thank You] Thank you for visiting us today

### B. Opening Greetings (書き出しの挨拶)
- **いつも大変お世話になっております。** (*Itsumo taihen osewa ni natte orimasu.* — Thank you very much for your continuous kind support.)
- **平素より格別のお引き立てを賜り、心より御礼申し上げます。** (*Heiso yori kakubetsu no ohikitate o tamawari...* — Ultra-formal opening for VIP clients or formal announcements.)
- **初めてご連絡を差し上げます。** (*Hajimete go-renraku o sashiagemasu.* — Contacting someone for the first time.)
- **突然のご連絡にて失礼いたします。** (*Totsuzen no go-renraku nite shitsurei itashimasu.* — Pardon the cold email/unexpected contact.)

### C. Mentioning Attachments (添付ファイルの案内)
- **添付ファイルにてお送りいたしますので、ご査収ください。**
  *(Tempu fairu nite o-okuri itashimasu node, go-sashū kudasai.)*
  — I send this via attached file; please examine and accept it.
- **資料を添付いたしましたので、ご一読いただけますと幸いです。**
  *(Shorui o tempu itashimashita node, go-ichidoku itadakemasu to saiwai desu.)*
  — I have attached the materials; I would be grateful if you could read through them.

### D. Making Polite Requests (依頼・確認)
- **ご確認のほど、よろしくお願い申し上げます。** (*Go-kakunin no hodo, yoroshiku onegai mōshiagemasu.* — We kindly ask for your confirmation.)
- **ご検討いただけますと幸甚に存じます。** (*Go-kentō itadakemasu to kōjin ni zonjimasu.* — We would appreciate your kind consideration.)
- **折り返しご連絡をいただけますと助かります。** (*Orikaeshi go-renraku o itadakemasu to tasukarimasu.* — It would be helpful if you could get back to us.)

### E. Standard Closing Sign-Offs (結びの挨拶)
- **何卒よろしくお願い申し上げます。** (*Nanitozo yoroshiku onegai mōshiagemasu.* — Standard, universal business sign-off.)
- **今後とも何卒よろしくご指導ご鞭撻のほどお願い申し上げます。** (*Kongo tomo nanitozo yoroshiku go-shidō go-bentatsu no hodo...* — Looking forward to your ongoing guidance and encouragement.)
- **取り急ぎ、メールにて御礼申し上げます。** (*Toriisogi, mēru nite o-rei mōshiagemasu.* — A quick note by email to express my gratitude.)

## 3. Traditional Japanese Letter Writing (手紙の作法)

Formal physical letters in Japan require matched pairs of **Opening Head Words (頭語 - *Tōgo*)** and **Closing Words (結語 - *Ketsugo*)**.

```
Opening Head Word (頭語) ────── Must match ──────► Closing Word (結語)
拝啓 (Haikei: "Humbly stated") ────────────────► 敬具 (Keigu: "Respectfully submitted")
前略 (Zenryaku: "Foregoing formalities") ──────► 草々 (Sōsō: "In haste / Briefly")
謹啓 (Kinkei: "Respectfully presented") ───────► 謹白 / 敬白 (Kimpaku: "Humbly spoken")
急啓 (Kyūkei: "Urgent opening") ───────────────► 早々 (Sōsō: "In quick haste")
```

| Letter Type | Opening Word (頭語) | Matching Closing Word (結語) | Usage Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Formal** | **拝啓** (*Haikei*) | **敬具** (*Keigu*) | Standard business letters, thank you notes, formal personal letters. |
| **Highest Politeness** | **謹啓** (*Kinkei*) | **謹言 / 謹白** (*Kingen / Kimpaku*) | Official announcements, corporate mergers, apologies, VIPs. |
| **Abbreviated / Informal** | **前略** (*Zenryaku*) | **草々** (*Sōsō*) | Skips the seasonal greeting; for urgent personal matters or informal inquiries. |
| **Urgent Notice** | **急啓 / 急呈** (*Kyūkei / Kyūtei*) | **早々 / 不一** (*Sōsō / Fuitsu*) | Sudden emergencies, urgent notifications. |
| **Reply Letter** | **拝復** (*Haifuku*) | **敬具** (*Keigu*) | Replying to a letter previously received. |

## 4. Seasonal Greetings by Month (季節の挨拶: Jisetsu no Aisatsu)

In formal letters (following **拝啓**), the opening paragraph begins with a seasonal greeting reflecting nature and climate:

| Month | Classical Name | Seasonal Opening Phrase (〜の候 / *...no kō*) | Meaning / Climate Note |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1月** | 睦月 (*Mutsuki*) | **新春の候** (*shinshun no kō*) / **初春の候** | Season of the New Spring / Fresh Year |
| **2月** | 如月 (*Kisaragi*) | **立春の候** (*risshun no kō*) / **余寒の候** | Beginning of Spring / Lingering Winter Cold |
| **3月** | 弥生 (*Yayoi*) | **早春の候** (*sōshun no kō*) / **春分の候** | Early Spring / Vernal Equinox |
| **4月** | 卯月 (*Uzuki*) | **陽春の候** (*yōshun no kō*) / **桜花の候** | Warm Spring Sunshine / Cherry Blossom Season |
| **5月** | 皐月 (*Satsuki*) | **新緑の候** (*shinryoku no kō*) / **薫風の候** | Season of Fresh Greenery / Fragrant May Breeze |
| **6月** | 水無月 (*Minazuki*) | **初夏の候** (*shoka no kō*) / **梅雨の候** | Early Summer / Rainy Season (*Tsuyu*) |
| **7月** | 文月 (*Fumizuki*) | **盛夏の候** (*seika no kō*) / **猛暑の候** | Height of Summer / Fierce Summer Heat |
| **8月** | 葉月 (*Hazuki*) | **残暑の候** (*zansho no kō*) / **晩夏の候** | Lingering Late Summer Heat |
| **9月** | 長月 (*Nagatsuki*) | **初秋の候** (*shoshū no kō*) / **新涼の候** | Early Autumn / Fresh Autumn Coolness |
| **10月** | 神無月 (*Kannazuki*) | **仲秋の候** (*chūshū no kō*) / **秋晴の候** | Mid-Autumn / Clear Autumn Skies |
| **11月** | 霜月 (*Shimotsuki*) | **晩秋の候** (*banshū no kō*) / **初霜の候** | Late Autumn / Season of First Frost |
| **12月** | 師走 (*Shiwasu*) | **初冬の候** (*shotō no kō*) / **寒冷の候** | Early Winter / Season of Severe Cold |

### Combining Seasonal Greeting with Health Wishes:
> **拝啓**
> **新緑の候**、貴社におかれましては**ますますご清栄のこととお慶び申し上げます**。
> 平素は格別のご高配を賜り、厚く御礼申し上げます。
> *(Haikei. Shinryoku no kō, kisha ni okaremashite wa masumasu go-seiei no koto to o-yorokobi mōshiagemasu...)*
> — Dear Sirs: In this season of fresh green foliage, we rejoice in the ever-increasing prosperity of your esteemed company...
