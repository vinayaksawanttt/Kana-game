# Fractions, Percentages, and Approximate Amounts (分数・割合・概数)

As you advance from JLPT N4 to N3, reading business charts, statistical graphs, survey results, and news articles requires fluency with **fractions**, **percentages**, and **numerical boundary markers**.

---

## 1. Fractions and Decimals (分数と小数)

### Fractions: [Denominator] 分の [Numerator]
In Japanese, fractions are read **bottom-to-top** (denominator first, then numerator), connected by **分の (ぶんの / *bun no*)**:

**Numerator / Denominator ⇒ [Denominator] 分の [Numerator]**

| Fraction | Japanese Kanji | Hiragana / Romaji | Literal Structure |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1/2** | 二分の一 | にぶんのいち (*nibun no ichi*) | 2 parts' 1 |
| **1/3** | 三分の一 | さんぶんのいち (*sanbun no ichi*) | 3 parts' 1 |
| **2/3** | 三分の二 | さんぶんのに (*sanbun no ni*) | 3 parts' 2 |
| **1/4** | 四分の一 | よんぶんのいち (*yonbun no ichi*) | 4 parts' 1 |
| **3/4** | 四分の三 | よんぶんのさん (*yonbun no san*) | 4 parts' 3 |
| **1/10** | 十分の一 | じゅうぶんのいち (*jūbun no ichi*) | 10 parts' 1 |

> [!NOTE] Half as a Standalone Noun
> While 1/2 is mathematically 二分の一 (*nibun no ichi*), the everyday noun for "half" is **半分 (はんぶん / *hanbun*)**.
> - ケーキを半分食べた。 (*I ate half the cake.*)

### Decimals (小数 / Shōsu)
Decimals use the word **点 (てん / *ten*)** for the decimal point:
- **0.5**: 〇点五 / 零点五 (*rei ten go / zero ten go*)
- **3.14**: 三点一四 (*san ten ichi yon*) — each digit after the point is read individually.
- **12.8%**: 十二点八パーセント (*jū-ni ten hachi pāsento*)

## 2. Percentages: Modern (パーセント) vs. Traditional (割・分・厘)

### System A: Modern Percentage (パーセント / Pāsento)
Attach **パーセント** directly to the number:
- **50%**: 五十パーセント (*go-jū pāsento*)
- **100%**: 百パーセント (*hyaku pāsento*)

### System B: Traditional Units (割・分・厘) — Vital for News & Shopping!
Traditional Japanese measures portions in descending powers of 10:
- **割 (わり / *wari*)** = **10%** (1/10)
- **分 (ぶ / *bu*)** = **1%** (1/100)
- **厘 (りん / *rin*)** = **0.1%** (1/1000)

| Fraction / % | Traditional Kanji | Reading | Commercial / Real-World Meaning |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10%** | 一割 | いちわり (*ichiwari*) | **1割引き** = 10% discount |
| **20%** | 二割 | にわり (*niwari*) | **2割引き** = 20% discount |
| **30%** | 三割 | さんわり (*sanwari*) | **3割引き** = 30% discount |
| **50%** | 五割 | ごわり (*gowari*) | Half (also called **半額 / はんがく** "half price") |
| **25%** | 二割五分 | にわりごぶ (*niwari gobu*) | 20% + 5% |
| **33.3%** | 三割三分三厘 | さんわりさんぶさんりん | Baseball batting averages / stats |

### Shopping Discount Keywords:
- **割引** (わりびき / *waribiki*): Discount (e.g., 2割引 = 20% off)
- **オフ** (*ofu*): Off (loanword, e.g., 20%オフ = 20% off)
- **半額** (はんがく / *hangaku*): Half price (50% off)
- **無料 / タダ** (むりょう / *muryō*): Free of charge (100% off)

## 3. Approximate Amounts (概数 / Gaisu)

| Expression | Position | Register & Nuance | Practical Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **約〜** (*yaku~*) | **Prefix** (before number) | Formal / News / Objective reports | **約500人** (*yaku gohyaku-nin* — approximately 500 people) |
| **〜くらい / ぐらい** (*kurai / gurai*) | **Suffix** (after number) | Standard conversational / Everyday | **千円くらい** (*sen-en kurai* — around 1,000 yen) |
| **〜ほど** (*hodo*) | **Suffix** (after number) | Formal / Polite written style | **二週間ほど** (*ni-shūkan hodo* — about two weeks) |
| **〜前後** (*zengo*) | **Suffix** (after number) | "Around / plus or minus" | **30歳前後** (*sanjussai zengo* — around 30 years old) |
| **〜程度** (*teido*) | **Suffix** (after number) | "To the degree of / roughly" | **10分程度** (*juppun teido* — roughly 10 minutes) |
| **〜強** (*kyō*) | **Suffix** | "A little over / slightly more than" | **50人強** (*gojū-nin kyō* — slightly over 50 people) |
| **〜弱** (*jaku*) | **Suffix** | "A little under / slightly less than" | **100万円弱** (*hyakuman-en jaku* — slightly under 1 million yen) |

## 4. Quantitative Boundaries: Inclusive vs. Exclusive Limits

A favorite trap in JLPT reading passages (instructions, regulations, timetables, and coupons):

```
       [未満] (< strictly less than)
───────┬───────────────────────────────>
       │ [以下] (<= and below)
───────┼───────────────────────────────>
   [Threshold]
───────┼───────────────────────────────>
       │ [以上] (>= and above)
───────┴───────────────────────────────>
       [超 / を超える] (> strictly greater than)
```

| Term | Reading | Boundary Condition | In English | Real-World Example |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **〜以上** | いじょう (*ijō*) | ≥ (Greater than or equal) | **Includes the number** ("X and above") | **20歳以上** (20 years old and older) |
| **〜以下** | いか (*ika*) | ≤ (Less than or equal) | **Includes the number** ("X and below") | **小学生以下** (Elementary schoolers and under) |
| **〜未満** | みまん (*miman*) | < (Strictly less than) | **EXCLUDES the number** ("Under X") | **18歳未満** (Under 18: ages 0 to 17 only) |
| **〜超 / を超える** | ちょう (*chō*) / こえる (*koeru*) | > (Strictly greater than) | **EXCLUDES the number** ("Exceeding X") | **100kgを超える** (Strictly over 100kg) |
| **〜以内** | いない (*inai*) | ≤ Limit | **Within the boundary** | **3日以内** (Within 3 days) |
| **〜以降** | いこう (*ikō*) | ≥ Date/Time | **From this point forward** | **17時以降** (From 17:00 onward) |
| **〜以前** | いぜん (*izen*) | ≤ Date/Time | **Before this point in time** | **2000年以前** (In or before the year 2000) |
