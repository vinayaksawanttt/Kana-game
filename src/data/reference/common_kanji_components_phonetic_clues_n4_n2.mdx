# Common Kanji Components & Phonetic Clues (形声文字 / Keisei Moji)

More than **80% of all Japanese Joyo kanji** are **Semantic-Phonetic Compounds (形声文字 / *Keisei Moji*)**. These characters are composed of two functional halves:
1. **The Semantic Radical (意味を表す部首)**: Usually positioned on the left or top, giving the **thematic domain** (water, person, hand, tree, speech, metal).
2. **The Phonetic Component (音を表すつくり)**: Usually positioned on the right or bottom, giving the **On'yomi pronunciation**!

By recognizing phonetic components, you can deduce the pronunciation of completely unfamiliar kanji on the JLPT N3, N2, and N1 exams.

## 3. Ideogrammic Compounds (会意文字 - Kaii Moji)

When a kanji is not phonetic, it is often an **Ideogrammic Compound (会意文字 / *Kaii Moji*)**, in which two or more distinct meaning components combine to tell a visual, metaphorical story:

- **休 (Rest)**: A **person (亻)** resting against a shaded **tree (木)**.
- **明 (Bright)**: The **sun (日)** and the **moon (月)** shining together in the sky.
- **看 (Watch / Nurse)**: A **hand (手)** placed above the **eye (目)** to shield against glare and peer into the distance (e.g., 看護師 *kangoshi* - nurse).
- **男 (Man)**: Physical **strength (力)** applied to cultivating the **rice field (田)**.
- **安 (Peace / Cheap)**: A **woman (女)** protected beneath the safety of a **roof (宀)**.
- **森 (Dense Forest)** & **林 (Woods)**: Two trees (**木+木**) make a small wood (**林**); three trees (**木+木+木**) create a deep forest (**森**).
- **集 (Gather / Collect)**: Several **birds (隹)** perched together atop a **tree (木)**.
- **鳴 (Chirp / Ring)**: A **bird (鳥)** opening its **mouth (口)** to sing.
