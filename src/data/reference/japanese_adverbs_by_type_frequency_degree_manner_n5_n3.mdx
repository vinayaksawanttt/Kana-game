# Japanese Adverbs by Type (副詞 / Fukushi)

In Japanese, **adverbs (副詞 / *fukushi*)** modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs to indicate **how (manner)**, **how much (degree)**, **how often (frequency)**, or **when (time)** an event occurs. Unlike English adverbs, most Japanese native adverbs do not require a special suffix like "-ly", though adjectives can be transformed into adverbs using specific conjugation rules:
- **い-adjective → -く** (e.g., 早い *hayai* → 早く *hayaku* "quickly / early")
- **な-adjective → -に** (e.g., 静か *shizuka* → 静かに *shizuka ni* "quietly")

---

## Word Order and Positioning Rules

1. **Flexible Placement**: Japanese adverbs generally appear before the word or clause they modify.
   - Typically placed right after the topic marker **は** or directly before the verb/adjective.
   - Example: 私は**いつも**朝ごはんを食べます。 (*I always eat breakfast.*)
2. **Emphasis**: Placing an adverb closer to the front of the sentence shifts focus to the manner or time.
3. **No Particles Required**: Independent native adverbs (e.g., たくさん, いつも, とても) usually do not take particles like に or を.

## 1. Frequency Adverbs (頻度の副詞)

Frequency adverbs describe the consistency with which an action takes place, ranging from 100% to 0%.

| Adverb | Kanji / Reading | Meaning / Rate | JLPT | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **いつも** | *itsumo* | Always (100%) | N5 | いつも7時に起きます。<br>*I always wake up at 7 o'clock.* |
| **たいてい** | 大抵 (*taitei*) | Usually / Mostly (~80-90%) | N5 | 休日はたいてい家にいます。<br>*On days off I'm usually at home.* |
| **よく** | *yoku* | Often / Frequently (~70%) | N5 | このカフェによく来ます。<br>*I often come to this cafe.* |
| **しばしば** | 屡々 (*shibashiba*) | Often / Repeatedly (formal) | N3 | しばしば海外出張へ行きます。<br>*He frequently goes on overseas business trips.* |
| **時々** | ときどき (*tokidoki*) | Sometimes (~50%) | N5 | 時々日本料理を作ります。<br>*I sometimes cook Japanese food.* |
| **たまに** | 偶に (*tamani*) | Occasionally / Once in a while (~20%) | N4 | たまに映画を見に行きます。<br>*I occasionally go to watch movies.* |
| **滅多に〜ない** | めったに (*mettani... nai*) | Rarely / Seldom (~5%) **[Neg]** | N3 | お酒は滅多に飲みません。<br>*I rarely drink alcohol.* |
| **あまり〜ない** | *amari... nai* | Not very often / Not much **[Neg]** | N5 | 朝ごはんはあまり食べません。<br>*I don't eat breakfast very often.* |
| **全然〜ない** | ぜんぜん (*zenzen... nai*) | Not at all (0%) **[Neg]** | N5 | 運動は全然しません。<br>*I do not exercise at all.* |
| **決して〜ない** | けっして (*kesshite... nai*) | By no means / Never **[Neg]** | N3 | 約束は決して破りません。<br>*I will never break my promise.* |

> [!WARNING] Negative Concordance (呼応の副詞)
> Adverbs like **あまり**, **滅多に**, **全然**, and **決して** require the sentence to conclude in a **negative predicate** (〜ない / 〜ません). Using them with positive predicates (except colloquial modern slang for 全然) is grammatically incorrect on the JLPT.

## 2. Degree and Quantity Adverbs (程度の副詞)

Degree adverbs specify intensity, proportion, or quantity.

| Adverb | Reading | Meaning | JLPT | Nuance / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **とても** | *totemo* | Very / Extremely | N5 | Neutral, universally applicable |
| **大変** | たいへん (*taihen*) | Terribly / Immensely | N5 | Formal, stresses difficulty or magnitude |
| **すごく** | *sugoku* | Incredibly / Very | N4 | Conversational, emphatic |
| **ずいぶん** | 随分 (*zuibun*) | Considerably / Quite | N4 | Exceeds speaker's initial expectation |
| **かなり** | 可成り (*kanari*) | Fairly / Pretty / Substantially | N3 | Objective degree, above average |
| **極めて** | きわめて (*kiwamete*) | Extremely / In the utmost | N2/N3 | Formal written or academic tone |
| **少し / ちょっと** | すこし / *chotto* | A little / Slightly | N5 | ちょっと is conversational; 少し is polite |
| **少々** | しょうしょう (*shōshō*) | A little / Momentarily | N3 | Polite business & service Japanese |
| **ずっと** | *zutto* | By far / Much more; All along | N4 | Used for drastic comparisons or continuity |
| **もっと** | *motto* | More | N5 | Comparative addition (*more than now*) |
| **ほぼ** | 略 (*hobo*) | Almost / Nearly (~95%) | N3 | Objective estimation (*hobo kansei*) |
| **ほとんど** | 殆ど (*hotondo*) | Almost all; (w/ Neg) Scarcely | N4 | ほとんど全員 (nearly all) / ほとんどない (almost none) |
| **完全に** | かんぜんに (*kanzen ni*) | Completely / Perfectly | N3 | Absolute state |

### Comparison Nuances: Quite vs. Extremely
- **かなり (kanari)**: Moderately high level based on objective evaluation (*kanari samui* = colder than normal).
- **ずいぶん (zuibun)**: Expresses surprise relative to prior expectation (*zuibun hayai desu ne* = You're surprisingly early!).
- **ずっと (zutto)**: Drastic gap in comparison (*A no hō ga zutto ii* = A is way better than B).

| **じっくり** | *jikkuri* | Deliberately / Thoroughly | N3 | じっくり考えて決めよう。<br>*Let's think carefully and decide.* |
| **そっと** | *sotto* | Softly / Gently / Stealthily | N3 | 赤ちゃんの部屋のドアをそっと閉めた。<br>*I gently closed the baby's room door.* |
| **さっと** | *satto* | Quickly / Swiftly / In a flash | N3 | テーブルをさっと拭いた。<br>*I wiped the table swiftly.* |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |

| **もう** | *mō* | Already; (w/ Neg) No longer; More | N5 | もう昼ご飯を食べましたか。<br>*Have you eaten lunch already?* |
| **まだ** | *mada* | Still; (w/ Neg) Not yet | N5 | まだ決めていません。<br>*I haven't decided yet.* |
| **すぐ** | 直ぐ (*sugu*) | Immediately / Right away | N5 | すぐ戻ります。<br>*I'll be right back.* |

| **いよいよ** | *iyoiyo* | At last / Finally (heightened anticipation) | N3 | いよいよ決勝戦が始まる。<br>*At last, the final championship game begins.* |

## 5. Outcome & Certainty Adverbs (結果・確信の副詞)

These adverbs express the expectation, outcome, or probability of an action.

| Adverb | Reading | Meaning | JLPT | Nuance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **やっと** | *yatto* | Finally / At last (positive relief) | N4 | Expresses relief after prolonged effort/waiting |
| **とうとう** | *tōtō* | At last / In the end (often negative/regretful) | N4 | Expresses final outcome after long progression |
| **ついに** | 遂に (*tsuini*) | Finally / Ultimately (grand / historic) | N3 | Used for momentous achievements or endpoints |
| **結局** | けっきょく (*kekkyoku*) | In the end / After all | N4 | Highlights contrast with what was originally planned |
| **やはり / やっぱり** | 矢張り (*yahari / yappari*) | As expected / After all | N4 | Confirms preexisting intuition |
| **たぶん** | 多分 (*tabun*) | Probably / Likely (~70-80%) | N5 | Pairs with だろう / と思う |
| **恐らく** | おそらく (*osoraku*) | In all likelihood / Probably (formal) | N3 | Formal/written probability marker |
| **まさか** | *masaka* | Never! / No way! / Surely not | N3 | Rejection of an unthinkable possibility |
| **ぜひ** | 是非 (*zehi*) | By all means / Definitely | N5 | Pairs with wish (〜たい) or request (〜てください) |
| **必ず** | かならず (*kanarazu*) | Without fail / Certainly | N4 | Inviolable commitment or natural law |

### Nuance Spotlight: やっと vs. とうとう vs. ついに
- **やっと**: Relief that something desired has finally happened.  
  *例: やっと試験が終わった！ (Finally, the exam is over!)*
- **とうとう**: An inevitable outcome occurred after a long time, often with a sense of regret or loss.  
  *例: 飼っていた犬がとうとう死んでしまった。 (Our dog finally passed away.)*
- **ついに**: A major, dramatic culmination of sustained effort or history.  
  *例: ついに宇宙船が月面に着陸した。 (At last, the spacecraft landed on the moon.)*
