# Four-Character Idioms: Yojijukugo (四字熟語)

**Yojijukugo (四字熟語)** are idiomatic compounds composed of exactly four kanji characters. Derived from classical Chinese historical chronicles, Buddhist philosophy, and traditional Japanese folklore, these compact phrases condense profound psychological insights, moral lessons, and strategic principles into four syllables.

Mastering Yojijukugo is essential for high scores on the **JLPT N2 and N1 reading and vocabulary sections**, as well as understanding formal Japanese journalism, speeches, and essays.
