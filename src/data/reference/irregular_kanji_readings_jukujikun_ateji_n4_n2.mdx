# Irregular Kanji Readings: Jukujikun & Ateji (熟字訓・当て字)

In standard Japanese compounds, each kanji contributes its own discrete On'yomi or Kun'yomi reading. However, two major classes of irregular vocabulary exist that **cannot be analyzed character-by-character**:

1. **Jukujikun (熟字訓)**: A preexisting native Japanese word (*Yamato-kotoba*) mapped onto a multi-kanji compound based purely on the **holistic meaning** of the combined characters.
2. **Ateji (当て字)**: Kanji selected primarily for their **phonetic sounds** to transcribe native or foreign words, regardless of the individual characters' literal meanings.

These words are heavily tested on the JLPT N4, N3, and N2 reading and vocabulary sections.

## 1. Time, Calendar & Chronological Jukujikun (N5–N3)

In these time expressions, you cannot assign parts of the reading to individual kanji:

| Kanji | Irregular Reading | Meaning | Literal Component Breakdown |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **今日** | **きょう** (*kyō*) | Today | Now (今) + Day (日) *(Formal: こんにち)* |
| **明日** | **あした / あす** (*ashita / asu*) | Tomorrow | Bright (明) + Day (日) *(Formal: みょうにち)* |
| **昨日** | **きのう** (*kinō*) | Yesterday | Previous (昨) + Day (日) *(Formal: さくじつ)* |
| **明後日** | **あさって** (*asatte*) | Day after tomorrow | Bright (明) + After (後) + Day (日) |
| **一昨日** | **おととい** (*ototoi*) | Day before yesterday | One (一) + Previous (昨) + Day (日) |
| **今年** | **ことし** (*kotoshi*) | This year | Now (今) + Year (年) *(Formal: ほんねん)* |
| **一昨年** | **おととし** (*ototoshi*) | Year before last | One (一) + Previous (昨) + Year (年) |
| **今朝** | **けさ** (*kesa*) | This morning | Now (今) + Morning (朝) *(NOT ima-asa!)* |
| **一日** | **ついたち** (*tsuitachi*) | 1st of the month | Derived from 月立ち (*tsukitachi* "moon rising") |
| **二十日** | **はつか** (*hatsuka*) | 20th day of month | Twenty (二十) + Day (日) |
| **大晦日** | **おおみそか** (*ōmisoka*) | New Year's Eve (Dec 31) | Great (大) + Last day of month (晦日) |

## 4. Common Conversational Ateji & Idiomatic Kanji (N3–N2)

In these expressions, kanji were either assigned for poetic phonetic value or fixed by centuries of literary convention:

| Kanji | Reading | Meaning | Nuance / Context |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **素敵** | **すてき** (*suteki*) | Lovely / Splendid / Wonderful | Extremely common aesthetic praise |
| **真面目** | **まじめ** (*majime*) | Serious / Diligent / Earnest | Describing a reliable personality |
| **面倒** | **めんどう** (*mendō*) | Tiresome / Bother / Hassle | 面倒くさい (*mendōkusai*) |
| **我慢** | **がまん** (*gaman*) | Patience / Endurance | Self-control in adversity |
| **出鱈目** | **でたらめ** (*detarame*) | Complete nonsense / Random | Groundless talk or actions |
| **流石** | **さすが** (*sasuga*) | Just as expected of... | High praise acknowledging skill |
| **矢張り** | **やはり / やっぱり** (*yahari / yappari*) | After all / As anticipated | Confirms prior hunch |
| **相変わらず**| **あいかわらず** (*aikawarazu*) | As usual / The same as ever | Standard greeting response |
| **滅多に** | **めったに** (*mettani*) | Rarely / Seldom | Always pairs with negative (〜ない) |
| **珈琲** | **コーヒー** (*kōhī*) | Coffee | Historical Meiji phonetic Ateji |
| **寿司** | **すし** (*sushi*) | Sushi | Auspicious Ateji (寿 = longevity, 司 = govern) |
