# Genki I & II — Dialogue Scene Locations & Background Image Prompts

This guide maps all **60 dialogue scenes** across **Genki I (Lessons 1–12)** and **Genki II (Lessons 13–23)** to their key locations, consolidated into **38 distinct background scenes** for use behind the Textbook Companion dialogue window.

---

## Global Style Guidelines (Use as Base Style for All Prompts)

> **Base Style Suffix (append to any prompt if your image generator needs consistent style):**
> `Japanese anime visual novel background art, clean modern Slice-of-Life aesthetic, Makoto Shinkai / Kyoto Animation inspired lighting, 16:9 wide aspect ratio, no characters, no people, no text, no watermarks, soft atmospheric depth so foreground UI and dialogue bubbles remain easily readable.`

## Part 2: Specific Scene Prompts for Image Generation

Copy and paste each prompt directly into your image generator. Every prompt avoids foreground characters so your character portraits and dialogue cards remain crisp and legible.

---

### 01. `01-university-orientation` (`l1-scene-i`)
> **Prompt:**
> Japanese anime visual novel background art of a bright, welcoming Japanese university campus plaza during new student orientation day. Modern brick and glass academic buildings, cherry or autumn-tinted trees lining a wide stone walkway, colorful orientation welcome banners and direction signboards in the background, clear blue morning sky with soft clouds, warm sunlight. Wide 16:9 composition, no people, no characters, clean readable foreground space.
